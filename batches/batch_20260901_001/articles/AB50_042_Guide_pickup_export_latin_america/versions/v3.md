# EN — English

## A badge does not tell you what the vehicle is legally

Pickups are marketed globally and classified nationally. The same double-cab diesel 4x4 can be a cargo vehicle in one country and a mixed passenger-and-cargo vehicle in another, and the difference changes who may ride in it, what it may carry, and which licence and tax regime applies. Start from the destination's legal definition, not from the brochure.

## Brazil: CAMINHONETE and CAMIONETA are different legal animals

Under Brazil's traffic code, CAMINHONETE is classified as a cargo vehicle with gross vehicle weight up to 3,500 kg, while CAMIONETA is a mixed passenger-and-cargo vehicle in the same compartment. A cargo vehicle may carry only two passengers besides the driver.

That last clause is where fleet buyers get caught: a double cab sold as a five-seater crew vehicle is only a five-seater if its legal classification allows it, and the classification is a matter of Brazilian law, not of how many seat belts are fitted.

Brazil also defines LOTACAO as the maximum useful load — kilograms for cargo, persons for passengers — and sets weight limits through CONTRAN resolutions, with cargo carried in passenger vehicles governed by separate rules. Together these are the legal basis for any payload boundary you intend to rely on.

## Peru: the used-pickup inspection gate

Peru controls used-vehicle imports through a technical sheet and inspection by a verifying entity, covering age, mileage, accident history, steering and emissions indexes. For a used pickup this is the admission gate, and it applies before any discussion of load capacity becomes relevant. A vehicle that fails an emissions index is not a cheaper truck; it is a rejected import.

## Argentina: model configuration licence for a new model

Argentina requires an LCM, plus LCA, for a new model to circulate, and recognises the Brazilian CAT through mutual recognition. For pickups sold from or through Brazil this is the model-configuration admission step — and it attaches to the exact model configuration, not to the nameplate.

## Payload and towing: require the qualifying configuration

Never treat a badge-level maximum towing or payload figure as the figure for the offered build. Require OEM data for the qualifying configuration plus the VIN or build label that proves the vehicle is that configuration. Cab style, bed length, engine, gearbox, axle ratio and drivetrain all move the number, and a brochure maximum usually belongs to a different vehicle.

## What a regional body cannot do for you

A regional bloc's own portal does not establish a pickup classification or admission rule, and a global type-approval framework does not classify a pickup under Brazilian or other Latin American commercial-versus-passenger law. Use them as background, never as the authority for a classification decision.

## Open items to close before payment

- Commercial-versus-passenger classification, licence class and load rules for Mexico, Colombia, Chile, Ecuador and other Latin American markets — not captured for this page.
- Any OEM towing or payload number for an offered build without the exact OEM derivative page.
- Fleet-safety purchasing guidance, regional-bloc pages, logistics indices and global type-approval frameworks: none of these sets a national classification, and MERCOSUR must not be cited as the classification authority.

## FAQ

### Is a double cab automatically a five-seater in Brazil?

No. Classification decides. A CAMINHONETE is a cargo vehicle and may carry only two passengers besides the driver; a CAMIONETA is the mixed-use classification.

### Can I rely on the brochure towing figure?

Only if it is the figure for the exact build. Require OEM data for the qualifying configuration plus VIN or build-label proof; cab, bed, drivetrain and axle ratio all change the number.

### Does an Argentine LCM cover the whole region?

No. It is Argentina's model-configuration licence for a new model; Brazil, Peru and Chile apply their own systems.

### What ends the check?

When the offered build's legal classification, its admission route in the named country and its payload evidence cannot all be documented, stop and request the missing document.

# ZH — 中文

## 铭牌不能告诉你车辆在法律上是什么

皮卡在全球销售，却按各国法律分类。同一辆双排座柴油四驱车，在一个国家可能是载货车辆，在另一个国家可能是客货两用车辆；这一差异决定了谁能乘坐、能装载什么，以及适用哪种驾照与税制。应从目的国的法律定义出发，而不是宣传册。

## 巴西：CAMINHONETE 与 CAMIONETA 是两种法律属性

根据巴西交通法，CAMINHONETE 被归类为总质量不超过 3,500 kg 的载货车辆，而 CAMIONETA 是同一舱室内的客货两用车辆。载货车辆除驾驶员外只能载运两名乘客。

最后这一条正是车队买家常踩的坑：作为五座乘员车出售的双排座车辆，只有在法律分类允许时才是五座车；分类由巴西法律决定，与装了几条安全带无关。

巴西还将 LOTACAO 定义为最大有效载荷（载货以公斤计、载客以人数计），并通过 CONTRAN 决议设定重量限制；客车载货另循专门规则。这些共同构成你所依赖的任何载荷边界的法律基础。

## 秘鲁：二手皮卡的检验关口

秘鲁通过技术表与核验机构检验管理二手车进口，检验指标包括车龄、里程、事故、转向与排放。对二手皮卡而言，这是准入门槛，且在讨论载重能力之前就已生效。排放指标不合格的车辆不是更便宜的卡车，而是被拒绝入境的货物。

## 阿根廷：新车型的车型配置许可

阿根廷要求新车型取得 LCM（另加 LCA）方可上路行驶，并通过互认承认巴西的 CAT。对从巴西或通过巴西销售的皮卡，这是车型配置准入环节——它依附于确切的车型配置，而不是品牌铭牌。

## 载荷与拖挂：要求对应配置的原厂数据

绝不要把铭牌级别的最大拖挂或载重数字当作所购配置的数据。必须索取对应合格配置的 OEM 数据，加上证明车辆确属该配置的 VIN 或车辆铭牌。驾驶室形式、货箱长度、发动机、变速箱、主减速比与驱动形式都会改变这个数字，而宣传册上的最大值通常属于另一辆车。

## 区域组织无法替你做什么

区域集团自身的门户不会确立皮卡的分类或准入规则，全球型式认证框架也不会依据巴西或其他拉美国家的客货分类法对皮卡进行分类。它们只能作为背景，绝不能作为分类决策的依据。

## 付款前必须关闭的开放项

- 墨西哥、哥伦比亚、智利、厄瓜多尔及其他拉美市场的客货分类、驾照类别与载重规则——本页未采集。
- 在没有确切 OEM 衍生车型页面的情况下，任何关于所售配置的 OEM 拖挂或载重数字。
- 车队安全采购指南、区域集团页面、物流指数与全球型式认证框架：这些都不设定国家分类，且不得把 MERCOSUR 引为分类主管机构。

## 常见问题

### 双排座在巴西自动算五座车吗？

不算，取决于分类。CAMINHONETE 是载货车辆，除驾驶员外只能载运两人；CAMIONETA 才是客货两用分类。

### 可以依据宣传册的拖挂数字吗？

只有该数字对应确切配置时才可以。必须索取合格配置的 OEM 数据，加上 VIN 或车辆铭牌证明；驾驶室、货箱、驱动形式与主减速比都会改变该数字。

### 阿根廷的 LCM 在整个区域通用吗？

不通用。它是阿根廷针对新车型的车型配置许可；巴西、秘鲁与智利各自适用自己的体系。

### 什么情况下核验终止？

当所售配置的法律分类、其在指定国家的准入路径以及载荷证据无法全部形成文档时，应停止并要求补齐缺失文件。
