# EN — English

## The question this page answers

A supplier sends one number — "USD 18,400 FOB Shenzhen" or "USD 21,900 CIF Tema" — and asks for a deposit. This page is the audit you run before that deposit leaves: what the trade term actually moves, where risk passes, what the import country will add on top, and which lines no quotation on earth can settle because they need a dated, named confirmation.

The two destination systems documented here are Ghana and Kenya. They are not interchangeable, and the difference is not cosmetic: Ghana and Kenya compute the dutiable base in different ways, and a rule copied from one to the other produces a wrong landed-cost model.

## What the Incoterm does and does not move

Under FOB the seller delivers once the goods are on board the buyer-nominated vessel, and risk normally transfers at that point. Under CIF the seller pays the main carriage to the named destination port and arranges insurance, but risk still transfers on board in China — not on African arrival. That single sentence is where most disputes start: a buyer reads "CIF Tema" as "delivered to Ghana" and discovers after a claim that the cargo was theirs from the moment it crossed the ship's rail in China.

Two variants matter for vehicle shipments. If a container is handed to a terminal before loading, FCA fits the transaction better than FOB. If you want the seller to arrange insured multimodal carriage, CIP at all-risk level is the matching rule, while CIF only requires minimum cover. Both statements come from the ICC Incoterms 2020 checklist, and neither changes who owns the goods at the discharge port.

## Customs value is national law, not the invoice

Invoice price is not automatically customs value. Whether freight is added into the dutiable base is determined by the importing country's national law, not by the Incoterm printed on the proforma. The WTO Customs Valuation Agreement supplies the global treaty basis for transaction-value valuation, but it is a framework: it does not tell a Ghanaian or Kenyan officer what to add to your number.

Practically, this means the same car with the same invoice can produce two different duty bases in two African ports, and a "CIF price" quoted by a supplier has no standing with the destination customs authority.

## Ghana: CIF-based assessment and the document set

Ghana assesses vehicle duty on a CIF basis. The authority applies age depreciation to the FOB figure, applies the exchange rate, and then considers freight and insurance. To check duty in ICUMS the importer must supply VIN, model, year and make — a vehicle that cannot be identified to that level cannot be valued.

Ghana clearing documents include the Bill of Lading or Waybill plus title. Local shipping-line and terminal handling charges are third-party destination charges: they are real costs, they are not in a FOB quote, and they are frequently the reason a "cheap" offer lands expensive.

## Kenya: CRSP schedule and the conformity gate

Kenya applies a Current Retail Selling Price (CRSP) schedule to compute used-vehicle customs value, effective 1 July 2025. The published schedule is an official reference table, which is exactly why a used-car quote can be tested against something other than the seller's word.

Kenya also requires import quality inspection. Non-compliant imports are prohibited and returned, and inspection runs through pre-shipment and port-of-entry routes. A vehicle that fails this gate is not a discount problem; it is a re-export problem.

Kenya's CRSP is Kenya-only and cannot be used as Ghana, South Africa or Nigeria valuation practice.

## Route cost blocks and who owns each line

Build the quote into five blocks and name an owner for each: (1) vehicle price and inland China collection, (2) export handling and main carriage, (3) marine insurance, (4) discharge, terminal and destination agency charges, (5) duty, taxes, registration and inland delivery. Any line without a dated written quotation stays marked open — never zero. Live ocean and inland freight, storage, detention, border transit and FX costs belong in that open column until a forwarder prices them for your sailing.

## Open items to close before payment

- Duty, VAT and levy percentages beyond what the Ghana Revenue Authority publishes for Ghana; no other African country's rate has been captured.
- Live freight, storage, detention, transit and FX costs.
- RoRo, container, EV and damaged-vehicle acceptance — carrier- and sailing-specific, and no carrier primary document has been captured.
- Valuation practice in Nigeria, South Africa or any third African country.

Treat each of these as a written question to the supplier, forwarder or authority, with a date and a named respondent. None of them may be written into the article or your costing as a settled number.

## FAQ

### Does CIF cover the duty in Tema or Mombasa?

No. CIF covers main carriage to the named port and minimum insurance. Duty, taxes, terminal charges, agency fees and inland delivery remain destination costs.

### Can I use the Kenyan CRSP table for a Ghana shipment?

No. Kenya's CRSP is a Kenya-only instrument. Ghana computes duty on a CIF basis with age depreciation applied to FOB and its own ICUMS check.

### Why will the supplier not confirm total landed cost?

Because several blocks are not theirs to price. Ask for the term, the vessel or booking window, and the document set in writing; take freight, insurance and destination charges from your own forwarder and agent.

### What should stop payment?

Stop when VIN, model year, new-or-used status, the customs-value basis for the specific country, or the inspection route cannot be reconciled in writing by a named party.

# ZH — 中文

## 这页解决什么问题

供应商给出一个数字——“USD 18,400 FOB 深圳”或“USD 21,900 CIF 特马”——然后要求付定金。本页给出付定金前必须跑完的核对清单：贸易术语到底转移了什么、风险在何处转移、目的国会在之上叠加哪些费用，以及哪些项目任何报价都无法一次性结清、必须拿到带日期的书面确认。

本页记录的两个目的国体系是加纳与肯尼亚。二者不能互换，差异也不是表述问题：两国的完税基础计算方式不同，把一国的规则套到另一国会直接得到错误的落地成本模型。

## 贸易术语转移了什么、没转移什么

FOB 项下，卖方在货物装上买方指定船舶时完成交付，风险通常在此转移。CIF 项下，卖方支付至指定目的港的主运费并安排保险，但风险仍在中国装船时转移，而不是非洲到港时。多数争议就出在这句话上：买方把“CIF 特马”理解为“交付到加纳”，出险后才发现货物自中国越过船舷那一刻起就是自己的。

车辆运输还有两种更贴合的变体：集装箱在装船前已交到码头，用 FCA 比 FOB 更准确；希望卖方安排投保的多式联运，应匹配 CIP（一切险级别），而 CIF 只要求最低险别。以上均依据国际商会 Incoterms 2020 清单，且都不改变卸货港的货权归属。

## 完税价格是本国法，不是发票金额

发票价格不自动等于完税价格。运费是否计入完税基础由进口国本国法律决定，与形式发票上印的贸易术语无关。WTO《海关估价协定》为成交价格估价提供全球条约基础，但它是框架性文件，不会告诉加纳或肯尼亚的关员要在你的数字上加什么。

实务含义是：同一辆车、同一张发票，在两个非洲港口可能得出两种不同的完税基础；供应商报出的“CIF 价”对目的国海关没有任何约束力。

## 加纳：以 CIF 为基础的估价与单证

加纳按 CIF 基础核定车辆关税：主管部门先对 FOB 金额作车龄折旧、适用汇率，再计入运费与保险费。在 ICUMS 中核查税款必须提供 VIN、型号、年份和品牌——无法识别到该颗粒度的车辆无法估价。

加纳清关单证包括提单或运单以及车辆所有权文件。当地船公司与码头操作费属于第三方目的港费用：这些成本真实存在，FOB 报价中不含，而且往往是“低价”方案落地后变贵的原因。

## 肯尼亚：CRSP 表与合格性门槛

肯尼亚自 2025 年 7 月 1 日起适用现行零售价（CRSP）表计算二手车完税价格。该表是官方参考表，正因如此，二手车报价才有了卖方说辞之外的检验基准。

肯尼亚同时要求进口质量检验：不合格进口车辆禁止入境并被退运，检验分装运前与进境口岸两条路径。车辆若在这一关口失败，问题不是折扣多少，而是退运。

肯尼亚的 CRSP 仅适用于肯尼亚，不能当作加纳、南非或尼日利亚的估价做法。

## 五个成本块与责任归属

把报价拆成五个块并为每块指定责任人：（1）车价与中国境内提货；（2）出口操作与主运费；（3）海运保险；（4）卸货、码头与目的港代理费用；（5）关税、税费、注册与内陆派送。任何一行若没有带日期的书面报价，就保持“待补”状态，绝不能记为零。实际海运费、内陆运费、仓储、滞箱、过境与汇率成本，在你的航次由货代报价之前都留在待补栏。

## 付款前必须关闭的开放项

- 加纳税务局公布的加纳范围之外的关税、增值税与附加税税率；本次未采集其他非洲国家的税率。
- 实时海运费、内陆运费、仓储费、滞箱费、过境费与汇率成本。
- 滚装、集装箱、电动车及受损车辆的承运受理条件——取决于承运人与航次，本次未取得承运人一手文件。
- 尼日利亚、南非或其他第三个非洲国家的估价做法。

以上每一项都要形成书面问题，发给供应商、货代或主管部门，并记录日期与答复人。任何一项都不得作为已确定数字写入本页或你的成本模型。

## 常见问题

### CIF 是否包含特马或蒙巴萨的关税？

不包含。CIF 只含至指定港口的主运费与最低险别保险。关税、税费、码头费、代理费与内陆派送仍是目的港成本。

### 能否把肯尼亚 CRSP 表用于加纳？

不能。肯尼亚 CRSP 是仅适用肯尼亚的工具。加纳按 CIF 基础、对 FOB 作车龄折旧，并有自己的 ICUMS 核查流程。

### 供应商为何不肯确认总落地成本？

因为其中若干块本不属于他可控范围。应书面要求其给出贸易术语、船期或订舱窗口与单证清单；运费、保险与目的港费用应向自己的货代与代理索取。

### 什么情况应暂停付款？

当 VIN、年款、新车或二手车状态、具体国家的完税价格基础，或检验路径无法由指定责任方书面对齐时，应暂停付款。
