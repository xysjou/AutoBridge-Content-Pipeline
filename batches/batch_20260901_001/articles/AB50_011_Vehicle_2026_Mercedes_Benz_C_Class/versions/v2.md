## FAQ

### Is this automatically a Chinese vehicle export offer?

No. Confirm the VIN, production market, seller authority, export eligibility, and destination approval. “Made in China” and “available from China” are not interchangeable claims.

### Should I compare FOB and CIF?

Yes. FOB can exclude ocean freight, insurance, destination charges, and local taxes; CIF generally includes freight and insurance to the named port but still excludes many destination costs. Compare like-for-like Incoterms in a landed-cost worksheet.

### What should stop payment?

Stop when the derivative, VIN evidence, source-market identity, inspection result, certification path, warranty route, or cost assumptions cannot be reconciled in writing.
