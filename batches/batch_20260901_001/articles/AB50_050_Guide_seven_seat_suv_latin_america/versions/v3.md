# EN — English

## "Seven seats" is a marketing count, not a load case

A badge that says seven seats answers one question: how many seating positions were fitted. It does not answer whether seven adults plus their luggage can be carried within the permitted load, whether the third row has the restraint equipment the destination's safety assessment looks at, or whether that exact derivative can be registered. Those are three separate tests, and this page walks through each.

## What the regional safety protocol actually assesses

Latin NCAP's 2026-2029 protocol, effective 1 January 2026, adds rear-seat adult-occupant assessment, including rear belt pretensioners and load limiters and rear whiplash, and the weakest assessment box sets the total star rating. That is meaningful evidence that third-row and restraint content is assessed — and it is explicit that the protocol does not mandate a seat count.

Two consequences follow. First, a rating belongs to the tested, declared-market specification: a lower trim cannot borrow a higher trim's stars. Second, a good star result tells you about crash performance, not about whether your seven passengers and their bags fit inside the legal payload.

## Brazil: LOTACAO is the legal load boundary

Brazil defines LOTACAO as the maximum useful load — a number of persons for passenger vehicles, kilograms for cargo — and sets weight limits through CONTRAN resolutions. That is the legal basis for a full-load check: seven occupants, their luggage and the vehicle's own mass have to sit inside the declared capacity, not inside the brochure's seating count.

## Argentina: model approval attaches to the exact derivative

Argentina requires an LCM, plus LCA, for a new model to circulate, with whole-vehicle certification routed through WP.29 via CVHE and a Brazilian CAT routed through ARM. Approval attaches to the model configuration that was presented. A seven-seat derivative is a different configuration from a five-seat one, and the difference is not cosmetic when the certificate names a configuration.

## A load test you can actually run

Before signing, put the mission in writing and test it: number of occupants with approximate masses, luggage mass, fuel state, and any accessory or roof-load. Compare the total against the declared LOTACAO or payload for the exact derivative, taken from OEM data and the VIN or build label. If the number only works with the third row folded or with luggage left behind, the vehicle does not meet the mission.

Check the restraint inventory at the same time: third-row seat belts, head restraints and, where the protocol considers it, rear pretensioners and load limiters. These vary by market and trim on the same nameplate.

## What no regional or global material can decide

A regional bloc's certificate dataset, a global vehicle-regulation framework and global EV or used-vehicle background material do not set a seven-seat rule or a payload figure for a Latin American destination. Where those sources were not reopened for this page, no figure is quoted here.

## FAQ

### Does a five-star Latin NCAP result prove the third row is safe for adults?

It proves the tested specification performed to a protocol that, from 1 January 2026, includes rear-seat adult-occupant assessment. Check that the rated vehicle is the specification you are buying; a lower trim cannot borrow a higher trim's stars.

### Can I carry seven adults and their luggage if the brochure says seven seats?

Only if the total stays inside the declared load. Brazil defines LOTACAO as the maximum useful load and sets limits through CONTRAN; compare your real mission against the OEM figure for the exact derivative.

### Is the Brazilian CAT enough for Argentina?

Argentina recognises a Brazilian CAT through a defined route for model configuration, but an LCM plus LCA is still required for a new model to circulate.

### What ends the check?

When the exact derivative's restraint inventory, declared load capacity and admission route cannot be evidenced together, stop and request the documents.

# ZH — 中文

## “七座”是营销计数，不是载荷工况

标称七座只回答了一个问题：安装了多少个座位。它不回答七个成年人加行李能否在允许载荷内运输，不回答第三排是否具备目的国安全评估所关注的约束装置，也不回答该确切衍生车型能否注册。这是三项独立的检验，本页逐项说明。

## 区域安全规程实际评估什么

Latin NCAP 的 2026-2029 规程自 2026 年 1 月 1 日起生效，新增后排成人乘员评估，包括后排安全带预紧器与限力器以及后排鞭打测试，且由最弱评估框决定总星级。这有力地说明第三排与约束装置内容是被评估的——同时规程明确并不强制座位数。

由此有两个结论。其一，评级只属于被测的、申报市场的规格：低配不能借用高配的星级。其二，好的星级说明的是碰撞表现，而不是你的七名乘客与行李是否落在合法载荷之内。

## 巴西：LOTACAO 是法律载荷边界

巴西将 LOTACAO 定义为最大有效载荷——载客车辆以人数计，载货车辆以公斤计——并通过 CONTRAN 决议设定重量限制。这正是满载检验的法律基础：七名乘员、行李与车辆自身质量必须落在申报载额之内，而不是宣传册的座位数之内。

## 阿根廷：车型批准依附于确切衍生车型

阿根廷要求新车型取得 LCM（另加 LCA）方可上路行驶；整车型式认证通过 CVHE 走 WP.29 路径，巴西 CAT 通过 ARM 路径。批准依附于所提交的车型配置。七座衍生车型与五座是不同的配置，当证书载明配置时，这一差异绝非表面文章。

## 真正可执行的载荷检验

签约前把任务书面化并检验：乘员人数与大致体重、行李质量、燃油状态，以及任何加装件或车顶负载。将合计值与确切衍生车型的申报 LOTACAO 或有效载荷（取自 OEM 数据与 VIN 或车辆铭牌）对比。若只有在折叠第三排或留下行李时才成立，那么这辆车并不满足任务需求。

同时核对约束装置清单：第三排安全带、头枕，以及在规程关注范围内的后排预紧器与限力器。这些在同一铭牌下会因市场与配置而异。

## 区域与全球材料无法决定什么

区域集团的证书数据集、全球车辆法规框架，以及全球电动车或二手车背景材料，都不会为拉美目的国设定七座规则或载荷数字。凡本页未重开的来源，均不引用其数字。

## 付款前必须关闭的开放项

- 确切的 OEM 衍生车型规格：第三排安全带与头枕配置、整备质量与总质量、有效载荷，以及七座全占用时的行李厢容积。未采集到 OEM 衍生车型一手文件。

## 常见问题

### Latin NCAP 五星能证明第三排对成年人安全吗？

它证明被测规格在一项自 2026 年 1 月 1 日起包含后排成人乘员评估的规程中达到了相应表现。需核对该评级车辆是否就是你所购规格；低配不能借用高配的星级。

### 宣传册写七座，就能载七名成人加行李吗？

只有在合计值落在申报载荷之内时才可以。巴西将 LOTACAO 定义为最大有效载荷，并通过 CONTRAN 设定限制；应把真实任务与确切衍生车型的 OEM 数字对比。

### 巴西 CAT 在阿根廷够用吗？

阿根廷通过既定路径承认巴西 CAT 用于车型配置，但新车型上路仍需 LCM 与 LCA。

### 什么情况下核验终止？

当确切衍生车型的约束装置清单、申报载荷能力与准入路径无法同时形成证据时，应停止并要求补齐文件。
