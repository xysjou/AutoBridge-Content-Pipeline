# EN — English

## The main voyage and the last mile are two different prices

In Southeast Asia the quote that matters is rarely the ocean one. A China-to-Singapore or China-to-Port Klang price is the main voyage; the feeder or inter-island leg, the permit stage and the registration stage are separate money. This page separates those ledgers so a FOB or CIF number is not mistaken for a delivered, registerable vehicle.

## Where the term stops

FOB risk transfers on board; under CIF the seller pays the main sea carriage and arranges insurance, but risk transfers on board in China. FCA fits a container handed to a terminal before loading, and CIP fits insured multimodal carriage including feeder and inland legs. That last distinction matters here: an archipelago destination often has a leg after the main voyage that neither FOB nor CIF describes.

## Singapore: the customs value is not the vehicle price

Singapore customs value starts from CIF or transaction value and adds freight, insurance, handling and other charges incurred to deliver the goods to Singapore. A bare FOB vehicle price is therefore not by itself the taxable customs value — the additions are part of the base.

The import procedure runs in four steps: value assessment, approval, an In-Payment Duty plus GST permit through TradeNet declaring the vehicle as new, used or temporary, then clearance. Road registration afterwards is handled separately by the Land Transport Authority.

## Singapore's used-car boundary

Imported used cars must be no more than 3 years old at registration, pay a S$10,000 per-car surcharge, meet emission and noise standards including the VETL test, and arrive with a Bill of Lading plus an Inward Cargo Clearance Permit. From 1 January 2025, new diesel and diesel-natural-gas car registration is not permitted. A quote for a used vehicle older than the age cap is a quote for something that cannot be registered.

## Malaysia: the Approved Permit is its own gate

Malaysia requires a personal-vehicle Approved Permit through ePermit, effective 1 August 2019. It is a national import-licence stage independent of the Incoterm, and a Malaysian AP does not work elsewhere in ASEAN. Buyers who treat the AP as paperwork to be sorted on arrival discover that it is a precondition.

## Five ledgers, and the missed feeder

Keep five ledgers and reconcile each with a document: origin and inland China, main voyage, discharge and terminal, feeder or last-mile, and permits plus registration. Model the missed-feeder case explicitly — a missed connection in an archipelago route is not a minor delay when storage charges and permit validity windows are running.

## FAQ

### Is the FOB price the Singapore customs value?

No. Singapore starts from CIF or transaction value and adds freight, insurance, handling and other charges to deliver the goods to Singapore.

### How old can an imported used car be in Singapore?

No more than 3 years at registration, with a S$10,000 surcharge and emission and noise compliance. From 1 January 2025 new diesel and diesel-NG car registration is not permitted.

### Does a Malaysian Approved Permit help in Thailand?

No. The AP is a Malaysian national instrument effective 1 August 2019 and does not transfer across ASEAN.

### Who prices the feeder leg?

The carrier that actually sails it. Feeder and inter-island legs, shipper-owned container rules and EV acceptance must be confirmed in writing by that carrier.

# ZH — 中文

## 主航程与最后一公里是两个价格

在东南亚，真正重要的报价往往不是海运那一段。中国到新加坡或巴生港的价格是主航程；支线或岛际航段、许可阶段与注册阶段是另外的钱。本页把这些账本分开，避免把 FOB 或 CIF 数字误认为已交付、可注册的车辆。

## 术语止于何处

FOB 风险在装船时转移；CIF 项下卖方支付主航段海运并安排保险，但风险在中国装船时转移。集装箱在装船前交到码头适用 FCA；包含支线与内陆的投保多式联运适用 CIP。最后这一区分在本地区尤其重要：群岛目的地常常存在一段主航程之后的运输，而 FOB 与 CIF 都不描述它。

## 新加坡：完税价格不是车价

新加坡完税价格以 CIF 或成交价格为基础，并加上运费、保险费、操作费以及将货物运抵新加坡所发生的其他费用。因此，单纯的 FOB 车价本身并不是应税完税价格——这些加项是基础的一部分。

进口流程分四步：估价、审批、通过 TradeNet 申报车辆为新车、二手车或临时进口并缴纳关税与消费税（In-Payment Permit），然后清关。此后的道路注册由陆路交通管理局（LTA）另行办理。

## 新加坡的二手车边界

进口二手车在注册时车龄不得超过 3 年，需缴纳每辆 S$10,000 附加费，满足排放与噪音标准（含 VETL 检测），并需提供提单与进境货物清关准证。自 2025 年 1 月 1 日起，不再允许新的柴油及柴油-天然气乘用车注册。超过车龄上限的二手车报价，本质上是买了一个无法注册的东西。

## 马来西亚：准证（AP）是独立关口

马来西亚自 2019 年 8 月 1 日起要求通过 ePermit 申请个人车辆进口准证（AP）。这是独立于贸易术语的国家进口许可环节，且马来西亚的 AP 在东盟其他国家无效。把 AP 当作到港后再补的手续，往往会发现它是前提条件。

## 五本账与错过支线船的情形

保持五本账并逐项用单证核对：中国境内原产地与内陆段、主航程、卸货与码头、支线或最后一公里、许可与注册。并应显式建模“错过支线船”的情形——在群岛航线上，当仓储费与准证有效期同时倒计时，错过衔接并不是小事。

## 常见问题

### FOB 价格就是新加坡的完税价格吗？

不是。新加坡以 CIF 或成交价格为基础，并加上运费、保险费、操作费及将货物运抵新加坡的其他费用。

### 新加坡进口二手车可以有多旧？

注册时车龄不得超过 3 年，另需缴纳 S$10,000 附加费并满足排放与噪音要求。自 2025 年 1 月 1 日起，新的柴油及柴油-天然气乘用车不予注册。

### 马来西亚准证在泰国有效吗？

无效。AP 是马来西亚的国家工具，自 2019 年 8 月 1 日起实施，不能跨东盟使用。

### 支线航段由谁定价？

由实际承运的承运人定价。支线与岛际航段、货主自备箱规则与电动车受理条件必须由该承运人书面确认。
