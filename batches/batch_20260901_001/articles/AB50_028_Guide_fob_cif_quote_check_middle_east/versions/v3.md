# EN — English

## Three different questions hide inside one quote

A Middle East vehicle quote looks like one number. It is really three: what the trade term moves, whether the vehicle is admissible in that specific state, and what the landed total becomes after duty, testing and registration. This page takes them in order and keeps them apart, because conflating them is how a cheap FOB number becomes an expensive, unregisterable car.

## What the term moves

FOB risk transfers on board; under CIF the seller pays sea carriage and arranges insurance, but risk still transfers on board in China, not at Gulf arrival. FCA fits a container delivered to a terminal before loading, and CIP fits insured multimodal carriage. None of these rules says anything about whether Saudi Arabia, Qatar or the UAE will let the vehicle in.

## Customs value: the invoice is an opening bid

The invoice amount is not automatically the customs value. Qatar's published rules show how a national system actually works: freight follows carrier receipts, with an estimate from similar shipments if none exist; insurance follows insurer receipts, and where these are absent 1% is added to reach a CIF value. Customs may also doubt a declared value. A supplier's CIF number is therefore one input into a calculation the authority controls.

## Saudi Arabia: age limits, prohibitions and its own valuation

Saudi ZATCA imposes model-age limits — up to 5 years for light vehicles and up to 5 years of operational life for heavy trucks — plus an energy-efficiency and SASO inquiry. It prohibits salvage, flood, fire, structural-damage and right-hand-drive-converted vehicles. Valuation is its own: invoice where available, otherwise agent lists, with 10% per year depreciation capped at 50%.

That last clause is worth reading twice. A used vehicle without a defensible invoice will be valued by list, and the depreciation cap means the authority's number will not fall indefinitely with age.

## UAE: seven prohibited used-vehicle categories

The UAE MoIAT operates an imported-used-vehicle licensing service, and national rules prohibit the import, licensing and insurance of seven categories: burnt, scrapped, flooded, disassembled, beyond-repair, major-defect and comparable conditions. Re-export light vehicles are excluded from the service. A quote for a vehicle in any of those categories has no path to registration, whatever it costs.

## Gulf conformity does not replace national control

GCC conformity certification is not a substitute for each state's own controls, and a "GCC specification" marketing claim proves neither authenticity nor scope. Qatar, Saudi and UAE methods are national and cannot be applied across the Middle East.

## Release gates and line ownership

Three gates govern release: commercial (has the seller performed what the term promised), eligibility (will the destination admit and register this specific vehicle), and landed (can every cost line be named and owned). Assign one accountable party per line before the vessel sails.

## FAQ

### Does CIF Dammam include Saudi duty and SASO testing?

No. CIF covers sea carriage and minimum insurance. Duty, testing, registration and agent costs are destination items governed by Saudi rules.

### What happens if there is no insurer receipt for the insurance element?

Under Qatar's published instructions, 1% is added to reach a CIF value. The authority may also challenge a declared value.

### Is a "GCC specification" badge enough?

No. Marketing language does not prove conformity authenticity or scope, and national controls still apply state by state.

### When should I stop?

When the vehicle's eligibility category, the age limit for its class, or the valuation basis for the named state cannot be confirmed in writing.

# ZH — 中文

## 一份报价里藏着三个问题

中东车辆报价看起来是一个数字，实际包含三个问题：贸易术语转移了什么、车辆能否进入该具体国家、以及落地总额在关税、检测与注册之后变成多少。本页按顺序拆开并保持分离——把三者混在一起，正是一个便宜的 FOB 数字最终变成昂贵且无法注册车辆的原因。

## 术语转移了什么

FOB 风险在装船时转移；CIF 项下卖方支付海运并安排保险，但风险仍在中国装船时转移，而非海湾到港时。集装箱在装船前交到码头适用 FCA；投保的多式联运适用 CIP。这些规则都不涉及沙特、卡塔尔或阿联酋是否允许该车辆入境。

## 完税价格：发票只是起拍价

发票金额不自动等于完税价格。卡塔尔公布的规则展示了国家体系的真实运作方式：运费依承运人收据，若无则参照类似货物估算；保险费依保险人收据，缺失时按 1% 加计至 CIF 价值。海关也可对申报价格提出质疑。因此供应商的 CIF 数字只是主管部门掌控的计算中的一项输入。

## 沙特：车龄限制、禁止类别与自己的估价方法

沙特 ZATCA 设定车型车龄限制——轻型车不超过 5 年，重型卡车运营寿命不超过 5 年——并执行能效与 SASO 核查；同时禁止事故报废、水浸、火烧、结构损伤及右舵改装车辆。估价方法也是自有的：有发票按发票，无发票按代理商价目表，每年折旧 10%，上限 50%。

最后这一句值得读两遍：没有可采信发票的二手车将按价目表估价，而折旧上限意味着主管部门的数字不会随车龄无限下降。

## 阿联酋：七类禁止入境的二手车

阿联酋 MoIAT 运营进口二手车许可服务，国家规定禁止七类车辆的进口、上牌与投保：火烧、报废、水浸、拆解、无法修复、重大缺陷及类似情形；再出口轻型车不在服务范围内。属于上述任何一类的车辆，无论价格多少都没有注册路径。

## 海湾合格性不能替代国家管控

GCC 合格性认证不能替代各国自身的管控，“GCC 规格”的营销说法既不能证明真伪，也不能证明范围。卡塔尔、沙特与阿联酋的方法都是国家层面的，不能在整个中东通用。

## 放行关口与责任归属

放行受三个关口约束：商务（卖方是否履行了术语承诺）、准入（目的国是否受理并注册该具体车辆）、落地（每一项成本是否都能点名并归属责任人）。应在船舶开航前为每一行指定唯一责任方。

## 付款前必须关闭的开放项

- GCC 合格性的具体功能主张，包括证书与 VIN 上传机制，需待相关平台页面重新打开并原文引用。
- 其他中东国家（科威特、约旦、阿曼、巴林、伊拉克等）的进口、税收与注册规则。
- 实时运费、码头费、检测费与保险成本，以及阿拉伯语理赔处理——均因具体 shipment 而异。

## 常见问题

### CIF 达曼是否包含沙特关税与 SASO 检测？

不包含。CIF 只含海运与最低险别保险。关税、检测、注册与代理费用属目的国事项，依沙特规则执行。

### 若没有保险人收据，保险部分怎么办？

按卡塔尔公布的指令，加计 1% 以达到 CIF 价值。主管部门也可能对申报价格提出质疑。

### “GCC 规格”标识够用吗？

不够。营销表述不能证明合格性的真伪或范围，各国管控仍逐国适用。

### 什么情况下应停止？

当车辆的准入类别、其所属类别的车龄限制，或指定国家的估价基础无法书面确认时，应停止。
