# EN — English

## Two different things are sold as one

Sellers use "battery warranty" to mean two different things, and conflating them is the most expensive mistake in this category. The first is a written commercial entitlement: a document that says who owes you what, in which territory, from which date, for how long, and under which exclusions. The second is the physical condition of the pack: how much capacity it still holds and how evenly its cells are balanced. A healthy pack with no written entitlement leaves you paying for a replacement. A strong document on a degraded pack leaves you arguing about measurement.

Record both, separately, per VIN.

## Singapore: the registration gate a warranty-eligible import must pass

Singapore registration defines new versus used, caps imported used cars at 3 years old at registration, levies S$10,000 per car, requires emission and noise compliance, and requires a Bill of Lading plus an Inward Cargo Clearance Permit. Every one of those conditions applies before any warranty conversation becomes relevant: a vehicle that cannot be registered has no warranty claim to make.

The practical point for a warranty file is the classification. Whether a vehicle is treated as new or used is decided by Singapore's own rules, and a seller's description of "unused stock" does not settle it.

## Malaysia: whole-vehicle type approval before registration

Malaysia requires whole-vehicle Type Approval of every new model before registration, under the Road Transport Act 1987. That is the national technical-admission gate. A model without VTA is not registerable, and a warranty document for an unapproved model is a document about a car that cannot legally be put on the road.

## What to capture in the warranty file

For each VIN, record: the warranty-issuing entity, the territory in which claims can be made, the start date and the trigger event, whether the entitlement transfers on resale, the capacity-retention threshold that triggers a claim, and the exclusions — with flood and water ingress read line by line. Ask for the document, not a summary of it, and ask who accepts a claim in the destination country.

## Flood exposure needs competence, not a dashboard

After flooding, isolate the vehicle and have insulation resistance, seals, connectors and fault codes inspected by someone competent to work on high-voltage systems. A dashboard reading is insufficient. This matters commercially as well as safely: water-ingress exclusions are common, and a claim without a proper inspection record is a claim you will lose.

## Battery condition: measure, then record the method

Record state of health with the tool and method that produced it, plus the date and the cell-balance picture. Do not convert one percentage into a guaranteed remaining range, and do not accept a figure quoted without its test cycle.

## Open items to close before payment

- OEM battery-warranty term, capacity-retention threshold, transferability, flood and water exclusions, and diagnostic-acceptance terms: no manufacturer warranty primary document has been captured.
- Local workshop software access, parts stock and replacement-pack availability by country.
- Regional light-electric-vehicle guidance, global EV outlook battery material and the UN battery-durability regulation: the cited pages returned access errors this session, so no regional or global provision is stated here. A technical durability regulation is not a commercial warranty.
- National warranty and consumer regimes in Thailand, Indonesia, Viet Nam and the Philippines.

## FAQ

### Is a manufacturer's global warranty valid in Kuala Lumpur?

Do not assume it. Confirm the territory, the accepting entity and the claim route in writing, and confirm the model has Malaysian VTA.

### Does a 70% state-of-health reading trigger a replacement?

Only if the warranty document says so. Capacity-retention thresholds are contractual terms, not physical constants, and no OEM threshold has been captured for this page.

### What if the car was flooded before shipment?

Isolate it and have a high-voltage-competent inspection of insulation resistance, seals, connectors and fault codes. Read the water-ingress exclusion in the warranty document before making a claim.

### Can Singapore's 3-year rule be waived for an EV?

This page states the rule as published: imported used cars must be 3 years or less at registration, with a S$10,000 surcharge and emission and noise compliance.

# ZH — 中文

## 被当作一件事出售的两件事

卖方口中的“电池保修”实际包含两件不同的事，把二者混为一谈是本类目中最昂贵的错误。第一件是书面的商务权利：一份文件，说明谁欠你什么、在哪个地区、从哪一天起、持续多久、有哪些除外责任。第二件是电池包的物理状态：还剩余多少容量、电芯均衡情况如何。状态良好但没有书面权利，更换电池的钱要你自己出；文件漂亮但电池已衰减，你会陷入关于测量方法的争论。

两者都应逐 VIN 分别记录。

## 新加坡：可主张保修的进口车辆必须先过的注册关口

新加坡的注册规则界定新车与二手车，二手车在注册时车龄不得超过 3 年，每辆征收 S$10,000 附加费，需满足排放与噪音标准，并需提供提单与进境货物清关准证。所有这些条件都先于任何保修讨论生效：无法注册的车辆，根本谈不上保修理赔。

对保修档案而言，实务要点在于分类。车辆按新车还是二手车处理，由新加坡自身规则决定，卖方所谓“未使用库存车”的说法不能决定这一点。

## 马来西亚：注册前的整车型式认证

马来西亚依据《1987 年道路交通法》要求每个新车型在注册前完成整车型式认证（VTA）。这是国家技术准入门槛。未获 VTA 的车型不能注册，而未获认证车型的保修文件，只是一份关于一辆无法合法上路车辆的文件。

## 保修档案应记录什么

逐 VIN 记录：保修出具主体、可提出理赔的地区、起算日期与触发事件、转售时权利是否可转让、触发理赔的容量保持率门槛，以及除外责任——其中水浸与进水条款要逐行阅读。索取文件本身而非摘要，并询问目的国由谁受理理赔。

## 水浸需要专业能力，而不是仪表盘

水浸后应隔离车辆，并由具备高压作业能力的人员检查绝缘电阻、密封件、连接器与故障码。仅看仪表盘读数是不够的。这既是安全问题也是商务问题：进水免责条款很常见，没有规范检测记录的理赔注定失败。

## 电池状态：先测量，再记录方法

记录健康状态（SOH）时必须同时记录得出该数值的工具与方法，以及日期与电芯均衡情况。不要把某个百分比换算成有保证的剩余续航，也不要接受未标注测试工况的数字。

## 付款前必须关闭的开放项

- 厂家电池保修期限、容量保持率门槛、可转让性、水浸/进水免责与检测受理条款：未采集到厂家保修一手文件。
- 各国本地维修厂的软件权限、配件库存与电池包更换件可得性。
- 区域轻型电动车指南、全球电动车展望电池章节与联合国电池耐久性法规：所引页面本次返回访问错误，因此本页不陈述任何区域或全球条款。技术性耐久性法规不是商业保修。
- 泰国、印度尼西亚、越南与菲律宾的国家保修与消费者保护制度。

## 常见问题

### 厂家的全球保修在吉隆坡有效吗？

不要假定有效。应以书面确认地区范围、受理主体与理赔路径，并确认该车型已取得马来西亚 VTA。

### 健康状态 70% 是否触发更换？

只有保修文件这么写才算。容量保持率门槛是合同条款，不是物理常数；本页未采集到任何厂家门槛。

### 车辆在装运前被水浸过怎么办？

应隔离车辆，并由具备高压作业能力的人员检查绝缘电阻、密封件、连接器与故障码。提出理赔前先阅读保修文件中的进水免责条款。

### 新加坡的 3 年规则对电动车可以豁免吗？

本页按公布内容陈述规则：进口二手车注册时车龄不得超过 3 年，需缴纳 S$10,000 附加费并满足排放与噪音要求。
