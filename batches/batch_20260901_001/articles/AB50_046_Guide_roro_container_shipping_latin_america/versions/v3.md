# EN — English

## Read this first: what this page can and cannot tell you

Most comparisons of RoRo and container shipping are written as if a regional average existed. It does not. Whether a specific vehicle moves by pure car carrier or in a box, on which service, from which terminal, under which dangerous-goods and securing instructions, and at what free time, is decided by the carrier and the port authority for that sailing.

For this page, no Latin American port-authority primary document and no car-carrier primary document has been captured. Rather than fill the gap with a plausible regional picture, this page states the one verifiable rule in the baseline and then gives you the evidence list to complete with dated, named confirmations.

## The one rule you can rely on today

Incoterms 2020 distinguish the maritime rules FOB and FAS from the multimodal rules FCA and CIP, and they distinguish CIF's minimum insurance from CIP's all-risk level. That distinction frames who arranges carriage and who bears insurance responsibility at the handover point — and it applies whichever mode you choose.

It does not tell you which mode to choose. Mode selection is an operational question about the specific vehicle, route and service, and the answer lives with the carrier.

## The evidence list to complete before booking

Record the following, each with a source, a date and a named responder:

- **Ports and terminals.** Origin and destination terminals by name, plus any transshipment port. Ask the terminal or port authority, not a marketplace listing.
- **Service and route.** The carrier's actual service, rotation and schedule for your window. Carrier-specific, and a different carrier on the same trade will give a different answer.
- **Vehicle condition and operability.** Whether the vehicle rolls, steers and brakes under its own power, its fuel or battery state, and whether it is damaged. RoRo typically requires an operable vehicle; damage changes the option set.
- **Battery and fuel rules.** State of charge limits, fuel drain requirements and any electric-vehicle restrictions on the specific service.
- **Securing.** Who lashes, to which standard, and who signs. Global cargo-securing frameworks exist, but the carrier's and terminal's own instructions govern your sailing.
- **Keys, accessories and condition record.** Photographic condition record at handover, key count, and what travels with the vehicle.
- **Insurance.** Which perils are covered, at which level, from which point to which point.
- **Destination release.** Who releases the vehicle, against which document, and what storage or free time applies from which hour.

## Container specifics worth asking about separately

If the container option is chosen, the packed container's gross mass has to be verified under the SOLAS verified-gross-mass requirement. That is a global requirement attached to the container option; it does not identify any Latin American port, and it does not tell you which service to book.

## What global material cannot do for you

Logistics-performance indices, global vehicle-regulation frameworks, maritime-transport reviews, regional development-bank material and trade-valuation frameworks are background. None of them establishes a port rule, a carrier acceptance position, a schedule or a rate. They are useful for understanding the environment and useless as booking evidence.

## FAQ

### Is RoRo cheaper than container to South America?

This page will not answer with a regional average. Price depends on the service, the sailing, the vehicle's operability and the terminal; ask the carrier that would actually move it.

### Can a non-running vehicle go RoRo?

Ask the carrier. Operability is a common RoRo acceptance condition, and damaged or non-running units frequently move by container instead — but the rule is the carrier's, not a general one.

### Who is responsible for lashing?

Whoever the carrier's and terminal's instructions assign it to, and it must be signed for. Global securing frameworks set the context; the sailing's instructions govern.

### Does container shipping avoid the verified-gross-mass requirement?

No. The SOLAS verified-gross-mass requirement attaches to a packed container, which is exactly the container option.

## 请先阅读：本页能说什么、不能说什么

大多数滚装与集装箱运输的比较文章，写得好像存在一个区域平均值。并不存在。一辆具体车辆是走纯汽车运输船还是装箱、走哪条航线服务、从哪个码头出运、适用哪项危险货物与绑扎要求、免费堆存多久，都由该航次的承运人与码头当局决定。

就本页而言，未采集到任何拉美港口当局的一手文件，也未采集到任何汽车运输船承运人的一手文件。与其用看似合理的区域图景填补空白，本页选择陈述基线中唯一可核验的规则，然后给出需要由带日期、具名答复去补全的证据清单。

## 今天可以依赖的唯一一条规则

Incoterms 2020 区分海运规则 FOB、FAS 与多式联运规则 FCA、CIP，并区分 CIF 的最低险别与 CIP 的一切险级别。这一区分界定了谁安排运输、谁在交接点承担保险责任——无论选择哪种运输方式都适用。

它并不告诉你该选哪种方式。方式选择是关于具体车辆、航线与服务的运营问题，答案在承运人手里。

## 订舱前必须补全的证据清单

逐项记录以下内容，每项都要有来源、日期与具名答复人：

- **港口与码头。** 起运与目的码头的名称，以及任何中转港。应向码头或港口当局询问，而不是市场 listings。
- **服务与航线。** 承运人在你的时间窗口内实际提供的服务、挂靠顺序与船期。这是承运人专属的，同一航线上换一家承运人答案就不同。
- **车辆状态与可移动性。** 车辆能否依靠自身动力行驶、转向与制动，燃油或电量状态，以及是否受损。滚装通常要求车辆可自行移动；受损会改变可选方案。
- **电池与燃油规则。** 荷电状态限制、燃油排空要求，以及该具体服务对电动车的限制。
- **绑扎。** 谁负责绑扎、按什么标准、由谁签字。全球绑扎框架只是背景，承运人与码头自身的指令才约束你的航次。
- **钥匙、随车物品与状态记录。** 交接时的照片状态记录、钥匙数量，以及随车运输的物品。
- **保险。** 承保哪些风险、什么级别、从哪一点到哪一点。
- **目的港放货。** 谁放车、凭什么单证、从哪一时刻起算免费堆存或仓储。

## 选择集装箱时需单独询问的事项

若选择集装箱方案，装箱后的集装箱总质量必须按 SOLAS 的核实总质量（VGM）要求核验。这是一项与集装箱方案绑定的全球要求；它既不指明任何拉美港口，也不能告诉你该订哪条服务。

## 全球性材料无法替你做什么

物流绩效指数、全球车辆法规框架、海运评述、区域开发银行资料与贸易估价框架都属于背景材料。它们都不确立港口规则、承运人受理立场、船期或费率。它们有助于理解环境，但不能作为订舱证据。

## 付款前必须关闭的开放项

- 实际的拉美车辆码头——桑托斯、卡亚俄、圣安东尼奥、韦拉克鲁斯、萨拉特、卡塔赫纳等——及其码头或港口当局一手文件：未采集，检索只返回行业与弱层级材料。
- 实际的纯汽车运输船服务、挂靠、船期，以及对电动车、二手车与受损车辆的受理立场及其生效日期：未采集到承运人一手文件。
- 国际危险货物与绑扎的具体条款：所引页面本页未重开，因此不引用任何条款。
- 任何运费、免费堆存期、中转或受理日期。

## 常见问题

### 到南美，滚装比集装箱便宜吗？

本页不会用区域平均值回答。价格取决于服务、航次、车辆可移动性与码头；应向实际承运的承运人询价。

### 无法行驶的车辆能走滚装吗？

应向承运人确认。可移动性通常是滚装的受理条件，受损或无法行驶的车辆往往改走集装箱——但规则属于承运人，不是通用规则。

### 绑扎由谁负责？

由承运人与码头指令所指定的一方负责，且必须签收。全球绑扎框架只是背景，航次指令优先。

### 走集装箱可以避开总质量核验要求吗？

不可以。SOLAS 的 VGM 要求正是对装箱后的集装箱提出的，而那正是集装箱方案。
