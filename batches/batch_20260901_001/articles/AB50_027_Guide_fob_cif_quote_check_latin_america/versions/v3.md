# EN — English

## Start with the term, then leave it behind

FOB and CIF answer one narrow question: who pays what up to the ship's rail, and when risk moves. Under FOB, risk transfers on board the buyer-nominated vessel, and FOB and FAS are maritime-only rules. Under CIF the seller pays main carriage and arranges minimum-cover insurance to the named Latin American port, but risk still transfers on board in China. A container delivered to a terminal before loading fits FCA better; insured multimodal carriage fits CIP.

Then the Incoterm stops being useful. From discharge onwards, four countries in this comparison run four separate systems, and none of them reads your proforma invoice as the final word on value.

## Customs value is decided at destination

The invoice amount is not automatically the customs value, and how freight is treated is a matter of national law. The WTO Customs Valuation Agreement provides the global treaty basis for transaction-value valuation, but it is a framework only: it does not create a preferential rate or tell a Brazilian or Peruvian officer which figure to start from. The practical rule for a buyer is simple — treat the supplier's price as one input to a national calculation you do not control.

## Brazil: declaration, licensing and valuation run through Siscomex and Duimp

Brazil treats the import declaration, import licensing (Licenciamento da Importação) and valuation as domestic customs procedures handled through Siscomex and Duimp, independent of the Incoterm. A seller who says "CIF Santos covers everything" is describing the ocean leg, not the Brazilian declaration.

Brazil's process cannot be applied to Spanish-speaking markets. Brazil, Colombia, Peru and Chile maintain distinct declaration, licensing, valuation and registration systems; a broker who works one does not automatically work the others.

## Colombia: prior registrations and licences through VUCE

Colombia's VUCE processes prior import registrations, licences and authorizations electronically before import. That is a pre-arrival gate: the document exists before the vessel sails, and a missing registration is not curable by paying more at the port.

## Peru: homologation for new, technical sheet plus inspection for used

Peru requires new vehicles to carry a homologation registry or conformity link, and used vehicles a technical sheet plus inspection by a verifying entity, including emissions, before nationalization. This is the stage that decides whether a used unit can become a Peruvian vehicle at all, and it sits entirely outside FOB and CIF.

## Chile: customs release and road registration are two different events

Chilean first registration requires the customs document proving legal import and duty payment, or a franchise, plus the invoice and green tax. Customs release is distinct from road registration: clearing the first does not deliver the second, and a quote that ends at "CIF San Antonio" ends before both.

## Origin preference is a claim you have to prove

Shipment from China, or a generic certificate of origin, does not create a preferential rate on its own. A preference needs an applicable agreement plus the product-specific rule satisfied by that vehicle. Write it into the quote as a claim to be evidenced, not as a discount already earned.

## Open items to close before payment

- Live freight, terminal, storage and inland rates, plus carrier acceptance for the specific sailing.
- Duty and tax percentages, and preferential-origin eligibility for any HS line: a destination customs adviser must answer these for your exact vehicle.
- Import rules for other Latin American markets (Mexico, Argentina, Uruguay, Ecuador, Costa Rica, Panama and others) — not captured for this page.

## FAQ

### Is CIF cheaper than FOB for a shipment to Santos or Callao?

Not inherently. CIF bundles carriage and minimum insurance into the seller's price; FOB leaves you to buy them. Compare the same scope, then compare what each term leaves for you to arrange.

### Can one broker handle Brazil, Colombia, Peru and Chile?

Assume not until proven. The declaration, licensing, valuation and registration systems are national and procedurally different; Brazil's Siscomex/Duimp route does not transfer to Spanish-speaking markets.

### Does a Chinese certificate of origin give me a lower duty?

Only if an applicable agreement covers the vehicle and its product-specific rule is met. A generic certificate proves origin, not preference.

### When should I stop the payment process?

When the vehicle identity, the customs-value basis for the named country, the pre-arrival registration or homologation requirement, or the first-registration document set cannot be evidenced in writing by a named party.

# ZH — 中文

## 先弄清术语，然后放下术语

FOB 与 CIF 只回答一个窄问题：到船舷为止谁付什么、风险何时转移。FOB 项下风险在装上买方指定船舶时转移，且 FOB 与 FAS 仅适用于海运。CIF 项下卖方支付至指定拉美港口的主运费并安排最低险别保险，但风险仍在中国装船时转移。集装箱在装船前已交到码头，用 FCA 更贴切；投保的多式联运应匹配 CIP。

此后贸易术语就不再有用了。从卸货开始，本页比较的四个国家运行四套独立体系，没有哪一国会把你的形式发票当作价值的最终结论。

## 完税价格由目的国决定

发票金额不自动等于完税价格，运费如何处理属本国法律事项。WTO《海关估价协定》为成交价格估价提供全球条约基础，但仅为框架：它既不产生优惠税率，也不告诉巴西或秘鲁的关员从哪个数字起算。对买方的实务规则很简单——把供应商价格视为一个你无法控制的国家级计算中的一项输入。

## 巴西：申报、许可与估价走 Siscomex 与 Duimp

巴西将进口申报、进口许可（Licenciamento da Importação）与估价视为本国海关程序，通过 Siscomex 与 Duimp 办理，与贸易术语无关。卖方说“CIF 桑托斯全包”，描述的只是海运段，不是巴西的申报流程。

巴西流程不能套用于西语市场。巴西、哥伦比亚、秘鲁与智利各自维持不同的申报、许可、估价与注册体系；做熟其中一国的报关行，不代表能做另一国。

## 哥伦比亚：通过 VUCE 办理事前登记与许可

哥伦比亚的 VUCE 以电子方式在进口前处理事前进口登记、许可与授权。这是到港前关口：文件必须在船舶开航前就存在，缺失登记不能在港口靠多付钱补救。

## 秘鲁：新车需 homologation，二手车需技术表加检验

秘鲁要求新车具备 homologation 登记或合格性链接；二手车则需技术表，并由核验机构实施检验（含排放），之后才能完成国产化（nationalization）。这一阶段决定了一辆二手车究竟能否成为秘鲁车辆，且完全处在 FOB 与 CIF 之外。

## 智利：海关放行与道路注册是两件事

智利首次注册要求提供证明合法进口与已缴关税（或享受免税）的海关文件，加上发票与绿色税。海关放行与道路注册是分开的：通过前者不等于拿到后者，而一份止于“CIF 圣安东尼奥”的报价，在两者之前就已结束。

## 原产地优惠是一项需要举证的主张

从中国出运，或一份一般原产地证，本身并不产生优惠税率。优惠需要存在适用协定，且该车辆满足相应的产品特定规则。应把它作为“待举证的主张”写入报价，而不是已经拿到手的折扣。

## 付款前必须关闭的开放项

- 具体航次的实时运费、码头费、仓储费、内陆费率与承运人受理条件。
- 关税与税费税率，以及任何 HS 编码项下的优惠原产地资格：这些必须由目的国海关顾问针对你的具体车辆作答。
- 其他拉美市场（墨西哥、阿根廷、乌拉圭、厄瓜多尔、哥斯达黎加、巴拿马等）的进口规则——本页未采集。

## 常见问题

### 发往桑托斯或卡亚俄，CIF 一定比 FOB 便宜吗？

不一定。CIF 只是把运费与最低险别保险打包进卖方报价；FOB 则由你自行采购。应在同一范围下比较，再看各自留下了什么需要你自己安排。

### 一家报关行能同时处理巴西、哥伦比亚、秘鲁与智利吗？

在证实之前应假定不能。四国的申报、许可、估价与注册体系在程序上各不相同；巴西的 Siscomex/Duimp 路径无法移植到西语市场。

### 中国的原产地证能让我少交税吗？

只有在存在适用协定且该车辆满足产品特定规则时才可以。一般原产地证证明的是原产地，不是优惠资格。

### 什么情况下应暂停付款？

当车辆身份、指定国家的完税价格基础、到港前的登记或 homologation 要求，或首次注册所需单证无法由指定责任方书面举证时，应暂停付款。
