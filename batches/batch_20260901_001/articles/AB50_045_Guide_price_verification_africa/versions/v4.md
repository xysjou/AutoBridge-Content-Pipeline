# EN — English

## What this page is, and what it deliberately is not

This page is an identity and quote-scope check. It is not a freight, insurance or inland-costing exercise, and it does not build a corridor model. The job here is narrower and, in practice, more valuable: decide whether the number you have been given belongs to the vehicle you think you are buying, and whether the destination authority has an official reference basis you can test it against.

## Freeze identity before you compare anything

Separate the authority's fields from the buyer's checklist. Ghana's duty-check process asks for the VIN/chassis number, model, year and make; those are the government-required identity fields. As a procurement method, this page additionally freezes the exact trim, steering side, powertrain and new-or-used condition, and for a batch it is useful to hold a VIN list or range and clear seller accountability — those extra items are AutoBridge's own verification checklist, not fields the Ghana Revenue Authority states it requires. If a seller cannot even identify the unit by its VIN, there is no price to verify — only an offer about an unidentified object.

## Why the invoice is not the finish line

Invoice and quote price is not automatically customs value; national law decides how freight and other elements are treated. That makes a supplier quote a reference input rather than a landed fact. The WTO Customs Valuation Agreement provides the global treaty basis for transaction-value valuation, but it is a framework document and gives you no country-specific answer.

The Incoterms 2020 rules are used here for exactly one purpose: to label the delivery point, the allocation of specified obligations and costs, and where risk transfers, so a quote's scope is clear. They define neither ownership transfer nor the customs value, and they do not cost the corridor. A quote labelled CIF tells you main carriage and minimum insurance are seller-arranged up to the named destination; it tells you nothing about what the destination will add.

## Ghana: an official CIF-based reference basis

Ghana provides an official valuation reference path: assessment on a CIF basis with age depreciation, and a VIN-based duty check. That combination is what turns a supplier's number into something testable — you can ask what the authority's own basis would produce for the same vehicle and year.

## Kenya: a published CRSP table for used vehicles

The Kenya Revenue Authority announced a Current Retail Selling Price schedule on 30 May 2025, with the new schedule taking effect on 1 July 2025; it is used to compute used-vehicle customs value. That announcement-and-effective-date pairing is all this page asserts: the schedule is time-sensitive, and the captured evidence does not verify that the same edition remains the current table in a later year, so check the KRA schedule in force on the day before relying on it. For a used-car quote it is a reference point: a seller's asking price and the official schedule can be compared, and a large unexplained gap is a question to ask before money moves.

Kenya's CRSP is Kenya-only. It cannot be used as Ghana, South Africa or Nigeria valuation practice, and Ghana's CIF-based approach cannot be read into Kenya either.

## The conformity gate that can void any price

Kenya's KEBS pre-export conformity process is a compliance gate a quoted vehicle must clear. The captured KEBS page does not by itself state a single fixed disposal outcome, and this page does not put legal wording in KEBS's mouth: goods judged non-conforming are not placed on the market and may involve return arrangements, but the exact consequence for a given vehicle must be confirmed with KEBS and the appointed inspection agent. In practical terms, a unit that cannot evidence conformity is not ready for price comparison.

## Stop rule

Pause the transaction when the VIN, the legal import route, the inspection scope, any landed-cost line, or the service responsibility cannot be tied to one accountable party. A missing answer is not a risk to be discounted; it is an open item with a name attached.

## Open items to close before payment

- Other African countries' official reference-price tables — only Ghana and Kenya are captured here.
- Authenticity of any specific supplier price or quote for a specific VIN.
- Logistics-performance indices supply no vehicle reference price and are not evidence for any figure on this page.

## FAQ

### Is the supplier quote the customs value?

No. Invoice or quote price is not automatically customs value; national law decides treatment, and Ghana and Kenya each apply their own basis.

### Can I use Kenya's CRSP table in Accra?

No. The CRSP schedule is a Kenya instrument, announced 30 May 2025 and effective 1 July 2025; confirm the edition in force on the day, and note Ghana assesses on a CIF basis with age depreciation.

### Why freeze identity at the VIN, and who asks for each field?

Ghana's duty check asks for the VIN/chassis number, model, year and make. Exact trim, steering, powertrain and, for a batch, a VIN list or range are this page's own procurement checklist so the price cannot be swapped across builds; a model name alone cannot produce an assessable value.

### What ends the verification?

Either every open item has a named owner and a document, or the inspection gate cannot be evidenced. In the second case the correct action is to stop, not to discount.

# ZH — 中文

## 这页是什么，以及它刻意不是什么

本页是身份与报价范围的核验，不是运费、保险或内陆成本的测算，也不构建航线成本模型。这里的工作更窄，但实务中更有价值：判断你拿到的数字是否属于你以为在买的那辆车，以及目的国主管部门是否存在可用于检验的官方参考基准。

## 比较之前先冻结身份

应把主管部门字段与买方清单分开。加纳的税款核查流程要求提供 VIN/车架号、型号、年份与品牌，这些是政府要求的身份字段；作为采购方法，本页另外要求冻结确切配置（trim）、转向方向、动力形式与新车/二手车状态，批量采购时还宜持有 VIN 清单或区间并明确卖方责任——这些附加项是 AutoBridge 自己的核验清单，并非加纳税务局声明其要求的字段。若卖方连 VIN 都无法标识车辆，就没有可核验的价格，只有一个关于未识别对象的报价。

## 为什么发票不是终点

发票价或报价不自动等于完税价格，运费及其他要素如何处理由本国法律决定。因此供应商报价只是参考输入，不是落地事实。WTO《海关估价协定》为成交价格估价提供全球条约基础，但它是框架文件，给不出任何国别答案。

本页使用 Incoterms 2020 规则只有一个目的：标注交付点、特定义务与费用的划分，以及风险在何处转移，从而厘清报价范围。它既不界定所有权转移，也不决定完税价格，更不用于测算航线成本。标为 CIF 的报价说明主运费与最低险别保险由卖方安排至指定目的港，但对目的国会加什么没有任何说明。

## 加纳：以 CIF 为基础的官方参考路径

加纳提供了官方估价参考路径：以 CIF 为基础核定，适用车龄折旧，并通过 VIN 核查税款。正是这一组合让供应商的数字变得可检验——你可以问：对同一辆车同一年份，主管部门自己的基准会得出什么结果。

## 肯尼亚：已公布的二手车 CRSP 表

肯尼亚税务局（KRA）于 2025 年 5 月 30 日公告现行零售价（CRSP）表，新表自 2025 年 7 月 1 日起生效，用于计算二手车完税价格。本页只主张这一“公告日—生效日”配对：该表属时效信息，已采集证据并未核证更晚年份仍为同一版本，依赖前应核对当日有效的 KRA 表。对二手车报价而言，它是一个参照点：卖方要价与官方表可以对照，若差距大且无法解释，就应在付款前提出问题。

肯尼亚的 CRSP 仅适用肯尼亚。它不能当作加纳、南非或尼日利亚的估价做法，加纳的 CIF 基础也不能反向套进肯尼亚。

## 可能让任何价格失效的合格性关口

肯尼亚 KEBS 的装运前合格性程序是报价车辆必须通过的合规关口。所采集的 KEBS 页面本身并未给出唯一固定的处置结果，本页也不替 KEBS 拟定法律措辞：被判定不符合标准的货物不得在市场销售，并可能涉及退运安排，但某辆车的确切后果须向 KEBS 与指定检验机构确认。实务上，无法证明合格性的车辆尚不具备进入价格比较的条件。

## 停止规则

当 VIN、合法进口路径、检验范围、任何一项落地成本，或售后责任无法对应到唯一责任方时，应暂停交易。缺失的答案不是可以打折的风险，而是一项附有人名的待办。

## 常见问题

### 供应商报价就是完税价格吗？

不是。发票价或报价不自动等于完税价格，处理方式由本国法律决定，加纳与肯尼亚各自适用自己的基准。

### 能否在阿克拉使用肯尼亚的 CRSP 表？

不能。CRSP 表是肯尼亚工具，2025-05-30 公告、2025-07-01 生效，须核对当日有效版本；加纳按 CIF 基础并适用车龄折旧。

### 为什么要按 VIN 冻结身份，各字段是谁要求的？

加纳税款核查要求 VIN/车架号、型号、年份与品牌；确切配置、转向、动力形式，以及批量时的 VIN 清单或区间，是本页自己的采购清单，用于防止价格在不同配置间被调换；仅凭车型名称无法得出可估价的结果。

### 什么情况下核验终止？

要么每一项开放项都有责任人和文件，要么检验关口无法举证。后者的正确动作是停止，而不是压价。
