The China-market 2026 Qiyuan Q05 deserves a place on an urban buyer’s shortlist when the priority is a five-seat electric SUV with a relatively compact body and a documented charging routine. It is a more persuasive proposition for a household with dependable home charging, or a small fleet returning to a controlled base, than for a buyer hoping an unfamiliar public network will solve every operational problem. That is a sourcing recommendation, not a road-test verdict: this article evaluates published information and the decisions it supports. It does not claim that AutoBridge has driven, inspected or imported the vehicle.

## Make sure the offer is for the right Q05

The reference here is the China-market 2026 battery-electric Q05, specifically the Chinese-labelled “506激光Ultra+” configuration. The Q05 name alone is insufficient: earlier plug-in-hybrid products and later model-year offers must not be treated as this exact vehicle. Autohome and YooJia identify the reference variant separately. YooJia currently labels its model page discontinued, so an existing database entry is not evidence of current production, available inventory or an export allocation. ## What the published reference specification actually says

The configuration databases list a 120 kW front-mounted motor, front-wheel drive, a 51.9 kWh lithium-iron-phosphate battery and 506 km CLTC range for the reference version. They also list five seats, dimensions of 4435 × 1855 × 1595 mm and a 2735 mm wheelbase. These are China-market, trim-bound reference figures; they are not global specifications and do not identify an offered VIN by themselves. The current Changan model directory confirms the Q05 product family, but it is not period-specific proof for every feature in a 2026 trim. [1][4][5]

## Why it can work for an urban household

The useful attraction is the combination of a five-seat layout and a body shorter than many larger family SUVs. That makes it a candidate to assess for commuting, school runs and ordinary shopping rather than an automatic substitute for a larger people carrier. A buyer should still try the actual seating position, rear-door opening and luggage arrangement with the people and objects normally carried. Wheelbase alone cannot prove rear-seat comfort, and exterior width does not tell you whether open doors and mirrors fit a narrow garage. Someone who needs a third row should remove this reference version from the shortlist rather than stretch its five-seat specification into a different use case.

## Use the range number to ask better questions

The 506 km figure belongs to CLTC. It is not a promise of highway distance with the air conditioning running, and this article does not convert it into WLTP, EPA or an invented real-world estimate. For a household, compare the normal day’s distance, access to charging and the longest regular trip. For a fleet, add duty changes, loaded operation and time needed to return the car to service. The larger battery in this particular reference is meaningful only if it solves a genuine usage constraint. A less expensive offer with a different battery or trim cannot be evaluated by borrowing the 506 km figure. [1][4][6]

## Pay for equipment you will actually use

A high trim’s equipment list can distract from the basics. The reference configuration is not proof that every Q05 has the same driver-assistance hardware, seat functions, cameras or software. Identify which features the buyer values and request evidence from the actual car, including the relevant menus and operation. Treat driver assistance as assistance, not autonomous driving. Overseas app access, account binding, navigation, phone connectivity and update support deserve a practical demonstration in the intended destination. A long list of China-market features has little purchasing value if the functions essential to the user cannot be supported where the car will operate.

## Charging and service decide whether the shortlist becomes an order

Photograph the actual charging inlets and obtain the exact AC and DC specifications and supplied-cable details. Do not infer destination compatibility from an overseas advertisement for another Q05. A claimed short charging time needs its starting and ending state of charge, charger conditions and battery conditions; it does not mean every charging stop will take that time. Then identify who can diagnose the vehicle, obtain appropriate parts and resolve battery or software faults locally. Ask separately about warranty territory and who pays for transport or labour during a claim. None of those commercial arrangements is established merely by the model’s battery chemistry or the presence of a brand logo.

## Compare identified stock, not headline domestic prices

A Chinese retail guide price, a local dealer promotion and an export quotation describe different transactions. This article gives no FOB price, CIF price, freight estimate or destination tax assumption. Request a written quote for an identified configuration and condition, showing the delivery term, named place, included documents and excluded costs. If the offered vehicle is existing stock, reconcile production date, storage history, mileage, registration history where applicable, battery-condition evidence and outstanding campaigns. Discontinued status on a data page does not make a particular unit bad; it makes stock identity and continued support more important to the decision. [2][3]

## Who should shortlist it—and who should choose differently

Shortlist the reference Q05 if five seats meet the requirement, routine urban driving dominates, charging is dependable and there is a credible local service route. Its published dimensions and electric specification provide a useful starting point for that buyer, but the final preference should follow an actual quote and a vehicle check. Choose a different candidate if you need more seats, require a demonstrated long-distance operating pattern, or cannot establish suitable charging and repair support. Do not let a low asking price compensate for an unresolved import or registration question. The destination’s eligibility and approval requirements must be checked for the offered vehicle, not assumed from a China-market specification.

## What to send with a serious enquiry

Send the seller the destination country, intended household or fleet use, required steering position, normal route pattern and charging arrangement. Request a reply tied to the exact market, production date and trim, with VIN evidence when a car is allocated. Ask for battery and charging documentation, equipment photographs, a destination-service proposal and an itemised quotation. That creates a comparison between actual vehicles rather than a contest between screenshots. The recommendation is therefore specific: assess the 2026 Q05 BEV as a compact urban sourcing candidate, while making the availability, support and suitability of the particular unit the conditions of purchase.

## FAQ

**Is this the same as every Qiyuan Q05?**

No. This profile concerns the China-market 2026 BEV reference “506激光Ultra+”. Earlier hybrid products, other trims and later model years require their own specification checks.

**Can the car be expected to travel 506 km on every trip?**

No. The published 506 km value is CLTC. Weather, route, speed, load and vehicle condition prevent treating a laboratory-cycle number as a universal journey guarantee.

**Does a discontinued database listing mean an offered car should be rejected?**

Not automatically. It means current supply cannot be inferred from the listing. Verify the actual unit, its history and a workable support arrangement before deciding.
