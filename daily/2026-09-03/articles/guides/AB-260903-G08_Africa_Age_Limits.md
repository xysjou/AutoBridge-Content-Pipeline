## Read the Evidence Ceiling Before the Table
Age limits change, are enforced differently by port, and are frequently misquoted in freight-forwarder summaries. **No African customs/standards-body primary page was captured in this package, and two key figures conflict across sources.** This page therefore does **not** hand you a "final" number for every country. It gives a structured matrix with the **reported** value, its confidence, and the exact official-verification route. The conflicting cells are quarantined on purpose — shipping against an unverified age figure is how units get turned back at port.

## First: Know Which "Age" Is Being Measured
Before any number matters, establish the basis:
- **Manufacture year vs first-registration year** — some regimes count from manufacture, others from first registration, and a few cap the **gap** between them.
- **The reference date** — age at shipment, at B/L date, or at arrival; the answer can differ by months.
- **Vehicle-class differences** — passenger car, pickup/light goods, bus/heavy truck often carry different limits.
Confirm these three in the destination's official instrument; a single headline "X years" without basis is unsafe.

## The Reliable Verification Route (use it for every shipment)
1. **Primary instrument:** the destination **customs authority / standards body (e.g., KEBS-type body)** current import rule or gazette — not a freight blog.
2. **Fix the basis:** manufacture vs registration, reference date, and vehicle class.
3. **Steering-side legality:** e.g., Nigeria LHD, Kenya RHD — a right steering/left steering mismatch can be rejected regardless of age.
4. **Inspection/conformity gate:** Nigeria **DI**, Ghana **OBD-II**, Côte d'Ivoire **CoC**, and any destination-designated PSI body (see PSI guide) — confirm the mandated programme and inspector.
5. **Re-verify at order time and again before shipment:** age rules are time-sensitive; a rule valid at quotation can change before sailing.

## What This Guide Recommends Before Paying
- Treat the matrix as a **screening map**, then replace each cell with an **officially verified** value and date for the exact vehicle class.
- For **Kenya and Nigeria, obtain the primary-source answer in writing** before committing; do not use the conflicting 7/8 or 12/15 figures in a customer quote.

- Capture the **manufacture date and first-registration date** from the VIN/registration papers during PSI, so age can be computed on the correct basis.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is the Kenya limit 7 or 8 years?** Sources conflict (7 vs 8) and no primary page was captured — verify with Kenya's standards/import authority before relying on either.
**Is Nigeria's limit 12 or 15 years?** Sources conflict (12 vs 15); this guide quarantines both pending Nigerian official confirmation; destination inspection is also reported.
**Does one rule cover all of Africa?** No — every country sets its own age basis, steering rule and inspection gate; there is no regional "Africa" rule.
**What matters besides age?** Steering side (LHD/RHD), manufacture-vs-registration basis, vehicle class, and mandated inspection/CoC programmes.
**Why leave cells blank/conflicted instead of giving a number?** Because an unverified or conflicting figure causes port rejection; a flagged gap with a verification route is safer than a wrong certainty.
