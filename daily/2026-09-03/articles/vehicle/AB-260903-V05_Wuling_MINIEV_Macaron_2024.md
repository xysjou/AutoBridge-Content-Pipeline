## Buyer Context: Match the Product to the Use Case
The Hongguang MINIEV Macaron is a **micro urban electric car**, not a compact EV. Its value proposition is short-trip city mobility at a very low Chinese price point; its limitations are top speed, range, charging speed and cabin size. An exporter who positions it against B-segment EVs will create returns. This page covers the **3rd-generation Macaron, model year 2024 (REFERENCE MARKET: CHINA)**.
