The XPeng MONA M03 is XPeng's compact five-seat electric fastback sedan, launched in China in August 2024 on a front single-motor, front-wheel-drive layout with an unusually low drag coefficient. For an overseas EV buyer the discipline is different from an ICE car: the order must lock the battery capacity, the test-cycle range and the motor as one matched SKU, because the M03 is sold in several combinations that are easy to mix. This page is a Chinese-market reference; all ranges are CLTC and must not be compared with WLTP/NEDC numbers, the battery supplier is not asserted, and export-market charging standards require separate evidence.
## Body identity and the year-over-year length nuance
- Body: five-door, five-seat compact electric liftback/fastback, single front permanent-magnet motor, front-wheel drive (corroborated).
- 2025 reference dimensions: 4780 × 1896 × 1445 mm, wheelbase 2815 mm; drag coefficient Cd 0.194.
- The 2026 model lists a 4785 mm length — a small year-over-year change to match on the certificate, not a different platform.

Fast-charge peak power and charge time are not stated here because no exact-trim official figure was captured; obtain them from the OEM for the specific version rather than copying a review.
## Why "CLTC" matters for an export buyer
Chinese range figures use the CLTC cycle, which is more generous than WLTP and far more generous than EPA. An overseas buyer converting expectations should treat the 515–620 km CLTC numbers as China-cycle references and request destination-cycle or real-world data separately. Equally, a China-spec M03 uses the Chinese charging standard/connector; compatibility with the destination's public network and any export-market connector must be verified separately and is not assumed from the Chinese specification.
## Software, equipment and trim discipline
Higher trims carry XPeng's assisted-driving and cockpit features, but the sensor set, software functions and which features are enabled vary by SKU and by the Chinese-market software environment. For an export buyer this is a concrete risk: China-market head-unit language, app-store access, map data and over-the-air service region may not match the destination. Test the exact VIN's software and connectivity before ordering rather than relying on launch marketing.
## Price, steering and export boundary
The Chinese guide price is a time-sensitive domestic reference only, never an FOB/CIF export quote, and no export price is given here. The referenced Chinese car is left-hand drive; any RHD or export-market M03 needs separate OEM evidence, and its battery/range calibration should not be assumed identical to the China SKUs.
## Overseas buyer verification before deposit
1. Lock the full matrix: battery (51.8 or 62.2 kWh) → CLTC range (which of 515/502/620/600) → motor (140 or 160 kW) for one named SKU.
2. Confirm the matrix and the battery supplier question against the XPeng official sheet/MIIT entry; do not infer the supplier.

5. Check the 4780-vs-4785 length against the exact model-year CoC.

## FAQ
What battery sizes does the MONA M03 offer?
Chinese-market LFP packs of 51.8 kWh and 62.2 kWh (one independent source, confirm by SKU); the exact cell supplier varies by batch and is not asserted here.
What is the range?
CLTC range levels of 515, 502, 620 and 600 km, each tied to a specific battery–motor pairing; these are CLTC figures, not WLTP.
