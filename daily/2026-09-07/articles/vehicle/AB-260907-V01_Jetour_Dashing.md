The Jetour Dashing, sold in China as the Dasheng (大圣), is the compact five-seat SUV Chery's Jetour brand positioned around bold crossover styling. For an overseas buyer sourcing from China the central discipline is version control: the line spans the 2024 and 2025 model years with subtly different body dimensions and three distinct petrol powertrains, and a quotation that says simply "a Dashing" without year and engine is ambiguous. This page is a **Chinese-market reference** — it is not a global specification sheet, and any export-market Dashing must be evidenced separately. Where a figure rests on a single independent source rather than an OEM exact-trim sheet, it is flagged for VIN-level confirmation rather than presented as settled.

## Body identity and the year-over-year dimension change
