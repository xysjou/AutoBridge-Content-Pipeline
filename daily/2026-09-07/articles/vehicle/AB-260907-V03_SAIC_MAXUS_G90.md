The SAIC MAXUS G90 is SAIC Maxus's large (upper-mid) people-carrier, aimed at executive shuttle, family and fleet use. For an export buyer the defining question is not sporty performance but **configuration**: the G90 is a very long MPV offered in six- and seven-seat layouts with dual sliding doors, and this page locks to the Chinese-market **petrol 2.0T + 8AT front-wheel-drive** line rather than blending in electrified or overseas variants. This is a **Chinese-market reference**, not a global specification; right-hand-drive and export builds require separate OEM evidence, and single-source outputs are flagged for VIN-level confirmation.

## Body identity and footprint

- Body: five-door, six-/seven-seat large MPV, front-engine/front-wheel drive, with **dual power sliding doors** (cross-checked across independent databases).
- Reference dimensions: **5280 × 1998 × 1845 mm, wheelbase 3200 mm**.

A 5.28-metre body on a 3.2-metre wheelbase is the G90's core value proposition — three usable rows and a large cabin — but it also sets practical export constraints: verify destination registration rules for a vehicle this long, garage/ferry/container dimensions, and the licence category required to drive a loaded seven-seat version in the target market.

## Six-seat versus seven-seat — specify the layout, not just the model

The G90 is offered in **six-seat (2+2+2)** and **seven-seat (2+2+3)** configurations, and the choice changes both comfort and the type-approval seat count. Because the precise mapping of which trim carries which layout is single-source and changes by model year, buyers should:

- name the seat count and the **2+2+2 / 2+2+3** layout explicitly in the contract;
- confirm second-row captain-chair versus bench content for the chosen trim;
- match the seat count to the homologation certificate rather than a marketing image.

Cargo volume is quoted at roughly **1010.5–2179 L** as the third row is folded (single-source), illustrating how much luggage space depends on whether the third row is in use; treat the exact figure as trim-dependent.
