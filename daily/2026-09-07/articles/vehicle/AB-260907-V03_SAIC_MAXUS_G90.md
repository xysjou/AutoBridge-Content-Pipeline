The SAIC MAXUS G90 is SAIC Maxus's large (upper-mid) people-carrier, aimed at executive shuttle, family and fleet use. For an export buyer the defining question is not sporty performance but configuration: the G90 is a very long MPV offered in six- and seven-seat layouts with dual sliding doors, and this page locks to the Chinese-market petrol 2.0T + 8AT front-wheel-drive line rather than blending in electrified or overseas variants. This is a Chinese-market reference, not a global specification; right-hand-drive and export builds require separate OEM evidence, and one independent source outputs are flagged for VIN-level confirmation.
## Body identity and footprint
- Body: five-door, six-/seven-seat large MPV, front-engine/front-wheel drive, with dual power sliding doors (corroborated across independent databases).
- Reference dimensions: 5280 × 1998 × 1845 mm, wheelbase 3200 mm.
A 5.28-metre body on a 3.2-metre wheelbase is the G90's core value proposition — three usable rows and a large cabin — but it also sets practical export constraints: verify destination registration rules for a vehicle this long, garage/ferry/container dimensions, and the licence category required to drive a loaded seven-seat version in the target market.

## Six-seat versus seven-seat — specify the layout, not just the model
The G90 is offered in six-seat (2+2+2) and seven-seat (2+2+3) configurations, and the choice changes both comfort and the type-approval seat count. Because the precise mapping of which trim carries which layout is one independent source and changes by model year, buyers should:
- name the seat count and the 2+2+2 / 2+2+3 layout explicitly in the contract;
- confirm second-row captain-chair versus bench content for the chosen trim;
- match the seat count to the homologation certificate rather than a marketing image.
Cargo volume is quoted at roughly 1010.5–2179 L as the third row is folded (one independent source), illustrating how much luggage space depends on whether the third row is in use; treat the exact figure as trim-dependent.

## Price and export boundary
The Chinese guide price is a time-sensitive domestic reference only, varying by seat layout and promotion, and must never be turned into an FOB/CIF export quote; no export price is given here. The referenced Chinese-market G90 is left-hand drive. Overseas MAXUS G90 builds are not assumed identical in powertrain, seating or equipment without an OEM same-model statement — a regional G90 is a related build until proven the same.
## Overseas buyer verification before deposit
1. Lock seat count and 2+2+2 / 2+2+3 layout, and map it to the exact trim in writing.
2. Confirm the 2.0T 8AT output and front-wheel drive against the OEM sheet/MIIT entry.
3. Confirm no electrified variant has been substituted in the quotation.
4. Check destination rules for a 5280-mm seven-seat vehicle and the required licence category.
5. Obtain separate OEM evidence for any RHD or export-market G90.
6. Reconcile VIN across contract, invoice, B/L and CoC before the balance is paid.
## FAQ
How big is the MAXUS G90?
Reference dimensions are 5280 × 1998 × 1845 mm with a 3200 mm wheelbase — a genuinely large MPV.
Does it come in six and seven seats?
Yes, in 2+2+2 six-seat and 2+2+3 seven-seat layouts; fix the exact layout and its trim in the contract and homologation paperwork.
What engine does this page cover?
The Chinese-market 2.0T petrol at a referenced 172 kW/360 N·m with an 8AT and front-wheel drive; hybrid/EV derivatives are separate and not covered here.
Is it all- or front-wheel drive?
The referenced Chinese-market line is front-wheel drive.
Is there a right-hand-drive or export version identical to this?
The Chinese car is LHD; RHD/export builds require separate OEM evidence and must not be assumed identical in power, seating or equipment.
