The Deepal S05 is Changan's Deepal-brand compact SUV, and the first thing an export buyer must understand is that "S05" names two technically different vehicles: an extended-range electric (EREV, which carries a combustion range-extender plus a battery) and a battery-electric (BEV). They share a body and are both rear-wheel drive, but their battery sizes, the meaning of their range figures and their use cases are not interchangeable. This page is a Chinese-market reference; EREV electric-only versus combined range is kept explicitly separate, all ranges are CLTC, the battery supplier is not asserted, and export-market versions require separate OEM evidence.
## Body identity and packaging
- Body: five-door, five-seat compact SUV, rear-motor/rear-wheel drive, offered in EREV and BEV forms (corroborated).
- Reference dimensions: 4620 × 1900 × 1600 mm, wheelbase 2880 mm; a 159 L front trunk and 492 L rear trunk are quoted for the platform.
- Reference kerb mass: BEV around 1770 kg, EREV around 1835 kg (one independent source) — consistent with the EREV carrying an additional engine-generator set.
## EREV line: read "electric range" and "combined range" as different numbers
| EREV item | Chinese-market reference |
| --- | --- |
| Traction battery | ≈ 27.28 kWh |
| CLTC electric-only range | ≈ 200 / 215 km (by trim) |
| CLTC combined range (battery + range-extender) | ≈ 1234 km (by trim/year) |
The single most important communication point for an EREV is that the ~200/215 km figure is battery-only electric driving, while the ~1234 km figure is the combined distance once the range-extender contributes. Quoting 1234 km as "electric range" would be a material misstatement. The range-extender's exact displacement and fuel consumption are not stated here (no exact-trim official figure captured); they must be confirmed against the Deepal official sheet. An EREV still needs petrol to reach the combined figure and carries the servicing complexity of both an electric drivetrain and an engine.

The BEV has no combustion engine and its 620 km is pure-electric CLTC range — a different concept from the EREV's combined number. Other BEV SKUs' battery/range combinations are not detailed here because they were not fully evidenced; do not extrapolate the 68.82 kWh/620 km pairing to other trims. The battery-cell supplier is not asserted: supplier attribution varies and must be confirmed per batch with Deepal.
## Why the EREV/BEV split drives the buying decision
For an overseas buyer the choice is operational, not just technical. The EREV suits markets with thin charging coverage because it can run once the battery is depleted; it also needs fuel-grade and engine-emission compatibility checks. The BEV is simpler mechanically but depends entirely on charging-standard and connector compatibility in the destination. Both are Chinese-spec left-hand-drive, rear-wheel-drive cars; all ranges are CLTC and should not be compared directly with WLTP/EPA. The Chinese-market head-unit language, app ecosystem and connected-service region should be tested on the exact VIN before order.

2. For EREV, quote electric-only and combined CLTC range separately; confirm extender displacement/fuel use officially.

## FAQ
Is the Deepal S05 electric or hybrid?
Both exist as separate versions: an EREV range-extender and a pure BEV; they share a body and are rear-wheel drive but must not be specced as one car.
What does the 1234 km EREV figure mean?
It is CLTC combined range using battery plus range-extender; battery-only electric range is around 200/215 km — do not quote 1234 km as electric range.
What is the BEV 620 specification?
The 620 version references a ≈68.82 kWh battery, 620 km CLTC and a 200 kW rear motor (one independent source, confirm by SKU).
Are these WLTP ranges?
No — all are CLTC Chinese-cycle figures; convert expectations separately for the destination.
Who supplies the battery and is there RHD?
The cell supplier is not asserted (confirm per batch); the Chinese cars are LHD and any RHD/export build needs separate OEM evidence.
