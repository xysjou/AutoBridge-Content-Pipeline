The FAW Jiefang J7 is the seventh-generation flagship heavy semi-trailer tractor from China's largest heavy-truck maker, sold mainly as a high-roof cab-over 6x4 for long-haul line-haul work. The decisive specification discipline for an export buyer is generational: the J7 has been offered with a China-5 (National V) engine from its 2018 launch era and a newer China-6 (National VI) engine family, and combining the largest number from each into one "J7 specification" is precisely the error this page prevents. This is a Chinese-market reference, scoped to diesel; LNG and pure-electric J7 variants and any overseas J7 are outside this page, and one independent source outputs are flagged for confirmation.
## Generation one — China-5 (historical variant, not the current truck)
| Item | Chinese-market reference |
| --- | --- |
| Engine | CA6DM3-55E52 (China-5 / National V) |
| Output | 550 PS, around 2300 N·m |
| Gearbox | 12-speed manual |
| Example homologation | CA4250P77K25T1E5 |
| Era | 2018 launch-era line (historical variant) |
These figures describe the 2018-era China-5 J7. They must be labelled as a historical emission stage and must not be presented as the currently on-sale truck. A China-5 engine also raises a direct export question: many destination markets now require a newer emission stage, so a China-5 unit may not be registerable even where the truck itself is available. Confirm destination emission recognition before sourcing this generation.
## Generation two — China-6 (current line)

The China-6 family is the current line, but its precise horsepower/torque steps across SKUs are not fully evidenced here — "up to 600 PS" is a ceiling reference, not a single fixed rating for every truck. An order must name the exact CA6SX1 power rating, gearbox, axle ratio and fifth-wheel setup and close them against the Jiefang official specification or the exact MIIT homologation entry. Items such as the precise fifth-wheel load, axle ratios, fuel-tank capacity and kerb mass are not stated because no exact-SKU source was captured.
## Why "take the maximum of both" is wrong
A spec sheet that lists "J7: 600 PS, 2300 N·m" by cherry-picking the China-6 horsepower and the China-5 torque — or that pairs the 2018 homologation number with the newest engine — describes a truck that does not exist. The correct method is to hold the two columns apart and bind every figure to its emission stage and SKU:
| Dimension | China-5 (historical) | China-6 (current) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engine | CA6DM3-55E52 | CA6SX1 |
| Power | 550 PS | Up to 600 PS (step by SKU) |
| Torque | ≈2300 N·m | ≈2800 N·m (by SKU) |
| Status | 2018-era, historical | Current line |
| Export note | Verify destination emission acceptance | Verify exact SKU rating officially |
## Heavy-truck buyer checks: engine, gearbox, axle, GCW, cab, chassis
For a tractor the buyer's decision runs across six dimensions, and each should be fixed in writing rather than assumed from the model name: (1) engine emission stage and exact rating; (2) gearbox (manual vs automated) and number of speeds; (3) drive axle and ratio; (4) gross combination weight (GCW) the configuration is approved to pull; (5) cab (high-roof sleeper specification) and (6) chassis options such as air suspension and the hydraulic retarder referenced for selected China-6 builds. Obtain the GCW and axle-load ratings from the homologation certificate; they are not inferred from engine power.
## Steering, fuel and export boundary
The referenced Chinese-market J7 is left-hand drive, diesel. LNG and pure-electric J7 variants and any right-hand-drive or export-market J7 require separate OEM evidence and are not assumed identical to the China diesel line. A heavy tractor's destination suitability also turns on local emission rules, axle-weight limits, trailer-coupling standard and parts/service availability — all to verify before deposit.
## Buyer verification before deposit
1. Declare the emission generation (China-5 historical vs China-6 current) and never merge their figures.
2. Bind the exact CA6SX1 power/torque step, gearbox and axle ratio to the official/MIIT SKU.
3. Pull GCW, axle loads, fifth-wheel load and kerb mass from the homologation certificate.
4. Confirm destination emission-stage recognition (especially for any China-5 unit).
5. Confirm air suspension/retarder content and cab spec for the chosen configuration.
6. Obtain separate OEM evidence for LNG/EV, RHD or export J7; reconcile VIN across contract, invoice, B/L and CoC before final payment.
## FAQ
What engine does the J7 use?
Two generations must be kept apart: the 2018-era China-5 CA6DM3-55E52 at 550 PS/≈2300 N·m (historical), and the current China-6 CA6SX1 family at up to 600 PS/≈2800 N·m (exact step by SKU).
Is the 550 PS truck the current model?
No — 550 PS belongs to the China-5 2018-era historical variant; the current line is China-6.
What is the J7's maximum power?
The China-6 CA6SX1 is referenced up to 600 PS, but the precise rating depends on the SKU and must be confirmed officially; it is not a single universal figure.
What drive layout is standard?
A high-roof 6x4 tractor is the dominant layout, with air suspension and a hydraulic retarder optional on selected builds.
Are LNG, electric or right-hand-drive J7s covered here?
No — this page is the Chinese-market diesel reference; LNG/EV, RHD and export J7s require separate OEM evidence and are not assumed identical.
