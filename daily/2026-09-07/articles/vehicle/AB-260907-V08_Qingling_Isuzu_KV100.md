The Qingling Isuzu KV100 is a cab-over (flat-face) light truck built by Qingling Motors in China for the domestic "blue-plate" N2 category — vehicles with a gross vehicle mass at or below 4.5 t that can be driven under an ordinary light-vehicle licence in China. For an export buyer the key disciplines are regulatory and model-line discipline: pin the exact engine suffix and its China-6 emission stage, keep the KV100 separate from adjacent Qingling/Isuzu lines such as the newer 100P, and do not treat used-truck classified payload figures as factory data. This page is a Chinese-market reference and does not equate the Qingling-built KV100 with the global Isuzu N/ELF range without an OEM same-model statement.
## Class identity and body
- Class: N2 light truck, GVW ≤ 4.5 t (Chinese blue-plate category), cab-over (flat-face) layout, diesel (corroborated for model-line identity).
- Common cargo-body length: roughly 4.2–4.3 m for typical box/flatbed applications (one independent source) — the exact body length is a superstructure choice and must be matched to the specific chassis and its homologation.
Because light trucks are sold as a chassis-cab plus a fitted body, "a KV100" can carry very different boxes, stakes or refrigerated bodies. The buyer should treat the chassis specification and the superstructure as two separate verification items and confirm the finished vehicle's dimensions and GVW on the certificate.
## Powertrain — pin the exact engine suffix
| Item | Chinese-market reference |

| Engine | 4KH1CN6LB 3.0-litre diesel, China-6 (National VI) |
| Output | 120 PS / 290 N·m |
| Gearbox | MSB 5-speed manual (5MT) |
The 4KH1 family exists in more than one suffix with different outputs, and a second independent report references a different 4KH1 suffix rating. That is exactly why the full engine code — 4KH1CN6LB, not merely "a 4KH1" — must be written into the order and matched to the MIIT homologation entry. A China-6 diesel is calibrated for Chinese fuel and the National VI stage; destination fuel grade and emission recognition must be checked separately rather than assumed.
## Payload and kerb mass: classifieds are not factory data
Used-vehicle listings (a directory/listing classified source) show sample kerb masses around 2.55–2.91 t and rated payloads around 1.495–1.75 t across different bodies. These figures are not presented as specifications: they vary with the fitted body, are self-reported by sellers and cannot be used to size a load. The authoritative rated payload, kerb mass and GVW come from the exact chassis homologation certificate / nameplate. Obtain those before committing to a payload duty cycle.
## Adjacent lines and the global Isuzu boundary
Two identity traps matter:
1. The newer 100P (4KB1) is a different model line. Its engine and specification do not transfer to the KV100; do not blend 100P figures into a KV100 quotation.
2. Qingling KV100 ≠ global Isuzu ELF/N-series by default. Qingling builds under its Isuzu relationship, but platform and branding kinship do not establish that the Chinese KV100 is identical in specification, homologation, parts or warranty to an Isuzu Elf sold elsewhere. Under the model-identity rule, a related but distinct model ≠ the same model family without an explicit OEM statement; do not quote global Isuzu power, torque or durability figures for the Chinese truck.
## Steering and export boundary
The referenced Chinese-market KV100 is left-hand drive. Right-hand-drive eligibility, any factory export specification and overseas model naming require separate OEM evidence. For a working truck, also verify the destination's axle-load rules, the licence category for the finished GVW, and whether the China-6 engine is recognised (or needs a different emission build) in the target market.

## FAQ
What category is the Qingling KV100?
It is a cab-over N2 blue-plate light truck with GVW at or below 4.5 t, typically fitted with a roughly 4.2–4.3 m cargo body.
What engine does it use?
The referenced line uses the 4KH1CN6LB 3.0-litre China-6 diesel quoted at 120 PS/290 N·m with an MSB 5MT; pin the full suffix because other 4KH1 ratings exist.
