A truck crane is not a truck with a winch bolted on: it is a **special-purpose vehicle combining a carrier chassis with a purpose-built lifting superstructure**, and its value and safety are defined almost entirely by that superstructure's rated load chart. The Zoomlion ZTC250H is the 25-tonne-class member of Zoomlion's ZTC truck-crane line. This page deliberately provides an **identity-level** Chinese-market reference: it fixes what the model is and how the product family works, but it does **not** print boom length, lifting height, engine power, dimensions or a load-rating table, because no exact-model ZTC250H OEM load table was captured — and borrowing those numbers from an adjacent 25-tonne crane would be a serious, potentially unsafe error.

## Model identity: what is established

- **Manufacturer / line:** Zoomlion (中联重科), ZTC truck-crane series; the **ZTC250H** is the series' nominal **25-tonne-class** machine (single-source product-line evidence).
- **Vehicle type:** wheeled mobile (truck) crane = a truck-type **carrier chassis** plus a rotating **lifting superstructure** (telescopic boom, slewing structure, outriggers, hydraulics and load-control systems).
- **"25 t" is a nominal class rating**, not a guarantee that the machine lifts 25 tonnes at every radius or configuration — the safe lift at any working radius is set by the model-specific rated-load chart.

## Why adjacent 25-tonne cranes cannot fill the gaps

It is tempting to complete a datasheet by copying figures from a similarly-named machine — the Zoomlion **QY25H431**, a **QY25K**, or any other "25-ton" crane. That is exactly what must not happen:

- **Adjacent model number ≠ identical specification.** Different model codes denote different booms (length and number of sections), different carriers, different outrigger bases, different hydraulic systems and therefore **different load charts**.
- A boom length or maximum lifting height from the QY25 series does **not** transfer to the ZTC250H.
- A load chart is safety-critical: an overstated radius/capacity figure can cause an overturn or structural failure, and exposes the exporter to liability.

Accordingly, the following remain **blocked / to be obtained from exact-model Zoomlion documentation**, and are intentionally not estimated here: main-boom length and number of sections; maximum lifting height (and jib/fly-jib height); carrier engine model and output; overall dimensions and kerb/GVW; maximum travel speed; and the full radius-vs-rated-capacity load table.

## Chassis–superstructure split and the export boundary

Because the machine is two systems in one, an export buyer verifies both: the **carrier** (engine emission stage, steering, road registration) and the **superstructure** (lifting certification and load chart). The referenced Chinese-market machine is **left-hand drive**. Any right-hand-drive, region-specific or export-certified ZTC250H requires separate Zoomlion OEM evidence; a related Zoomlion crane is not assumed identical. Do not infer engine or chassis data from another Zoomlion model, and do not treat a generic "Chinese 25 t crane" specification as applying to this unit.

## FAQ
**What does "25 tonnes" mean for the ZTC250H?**

It is the nominal 25-tonne class; the safe lift at any radius is set by the model-specific load chart, not a flat 25 t in every configuration.

**Can I use a QY25H431 or QY25K load chart for the ZTC250H?**

No. Adjacent 25-tonne models have different booms, carriers and load charts; borrowing those figures is unsafe and is specifically excluded here.

**Why are boom length and lifting height not listed?**

No exact-model ZTC250H OEM data was captured, so these are deliberately withheld rather than estimated from similar cranes; obtain them from Zoomlion for the exact serial number.

**What makes a truck crane different to verify than a truck?**

It combines a carrier chassis (engine/emission/registration) with a lifting superstructure (load chart, outriggers, safety devices); both must be verified separately.

**Is there an export or right-hand-drive version identical to this?**

The Chinese-market machine is LHD; any RHD or export-certified ZTC250H requires separate Zoomlion OEM evidence and is not assumed identical.
