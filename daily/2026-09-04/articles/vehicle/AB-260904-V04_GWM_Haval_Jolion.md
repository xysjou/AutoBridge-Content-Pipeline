## Identity Boundary First (read this before any spec line)
"Jolion" is the **export-market name**. In China the related compact SUV is marketed as the **Haval Chulian (哈弗初恋)**. Under our sourcing rule an export name and a Chinese name are treated as a **RELATED_MODEL** relationship unless the OEM explicitly documents them as the same vehicle. **No GWM OEM statement proving one-to-one identity was captured in this research pack**, so this page does **not** assert "Jolion = Chulian, same model." It documents the **Chinese-market 1.5T car (REFERENCE MARKET: CHINA)** and lists overseas HEV figures strictly separately. That distinction is itself the main order-error risk on this nameplate.

## Overseas HEV — a Separate Specification, Not the Same Datasheet
Overseas markets (Australia and Vietnam coverage captured) offer a **Jolion HEV built around a 1.5L DHT hybrid system**, reported at roughly **190 PS combined and 375 N·m** (single-source, overseas-market). Australian coverage also lists cargo volume of **430 L / 1130 L** with the rear seats folded.
- These are **overseas-market figures (REFERENCE MARKET: overseas)** and must **not** be merged into the Chinese 1.5T specification, and the Chinese 1.5T must not be presented as a "global spec."
- The HEV uses a different powertrain architecture; quoting the 190 PS hybrid output for a Chinese 1.5T unit — or the 110 kW turbo output for an HEV — is a direct configuration error.

## How to Select Without Blending the Two
1. Decide whether the enquiry is for the **Chinese 1.5T line** or an **overseas-market HEV** before reading any number.
2. Record engine type (1.5T turbo vs 1.5L DHT HEV), power and transmission against the VIN — see the VIN/nameplate guide.
3. Keep market labels attached to every dimension and cargo figure; the 430/1130 L cargo number is the Australian version, not the Chinese car.
4. Confirm steering side and destination homologation; a Chinese LHD 1.5T car is not evidence of RHD or HEV availability.
5. Because the Jolion/Chulian identity lacks OEM proof here, ask the supplier for an **OEM model-code mapping** before using the two names interchangeably on a PI.
