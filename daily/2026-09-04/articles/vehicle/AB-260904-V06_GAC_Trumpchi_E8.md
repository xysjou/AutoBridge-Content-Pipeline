## Buyer Context: an MPV Purchase Is Decided by Seating and by the PHEV/HEV Split
For an MPV exporter the questions are cabin geometry and seat architecture first, powertrain second. On the E8 there is a naming trap: the E8 New Energy is a plug-in hybrid with a charging port, while the E8 Glory (glory trim) is an HEV with no charging port. They are different energy types and must be quoted on separate lines. This sheet documents the Chinese-market PHEV (Chinese-market reference); an OEM configuration page was captured.

## PHEV vs Glory HEV — Keep the Energy Type Visible
- E8 New Energy (this page): PHEV — external charging port, 25.57 kWh traction battery, 150 km CLTC electric running.
- E8 Glory (glory trim): HEV — no charging port; it self-charges and cannot be plugged in.
- Quoting PHEV electric range or charging for a Glory HEV unit (or pricing a PHEV against HEV equipment) is the recurring mismatch on this nameplate. Chinese guide prices also differ (PHEV from about RMB 209,800; Glory HEV from about RMB 166,800 — domestic, time-sensitive references only).
