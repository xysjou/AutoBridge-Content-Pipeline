## Xuất tài liệu (thực hiện, không phải bí danh)
China Transport News (an industry news outlet) reports that in **February 2024 an initial batch of EU-specification V6E vans was exported to Türkiye**, described as Farizon's first entry into that market, in 6 m³ form. Đây là một kỷ lục về thể thao **xexport-delivery**: it confirms a European-standard build was shipped, but it does **not** make the Türkiye EU-spec van identical to the China 35.2/51 kWh builds — treat export configuration separately and request its spec sheet.
