## Buyer context: a heavy tipper defined by chassis plus superstructure
The **Shacman Delong M3000S (德龙M3000S) dump truck** is sold in China-V and China-VI engine lines. A tipper is always a **chassis plus a tipping superstructure**, so payload and body dimensions are configuration-specific and cannot be quoted as one universal M3000S number. Reference market is **China**.
