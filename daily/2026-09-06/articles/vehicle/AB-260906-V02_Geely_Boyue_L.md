The Boyue L is the fourth-generation evolution of Geely's long-running Boyue (Atlas/Proton X70-family lineage) compact SUV — a five-door, five-seat, front-wheel-drive crossover aimed at the heart of the family-SUV segment. Sourcing it for export is mostly a question of choosing the right powertrain and not confusing the Chinese car with the differently specified export-market Starray. This page documents the **Chinese-market reference** only.

## Two petrol powertrains, different evidence strength

- **1.5T** — 133 kW (181 PS) / 290 N·m with a 7-speed wet dual-clutch, running on 92-RON petrol; cross-checked across independent Chinese databases.
- **2.0T** — 160 kW (218 PS) / 325 N·m with 7DCT; currently a single-source figure that should be confirmed against the OEM sheet/VIN before being written into a contract.

Do not blend the two: fuel grade, transmission calibration, equipment and price tier all differ. A buyer asking for 'the 2.0-litre Boyue L' should receive the exact SKU sheet, not a mix of 1.5T equipment and 2.0T power.

## The Starray caveat: export name, not identical spec

Geely markets an export SUV under the **Starray** name, and OEM regional brochures exist (for example Gulf-market Starray documents with their own dimensions and a 2.0T+7DCT regional calibration). Per model-identity rules, an export nameplate does not establish point-by-point equivalence with the Chinese Boyue L: regional length, wheelbase and power can differ. Treat Starray material as export-market evidence and request the exact destination OEM sheet rather than copying Chinese numbers onto it.

## Interior and practicality buyers ask about

As a family SUV the Boyue L value story is cabin space, the 650-to-1610-litre cargo flexibility and the dual-screen cockpit. Because trim levels change the screen, seat and driver-assist content by model year, specify the configuration line (and production date) on the proforma; a promotional equipment list is not a build sheet.

## Domestic reference price only

Any Chinese guide price is a domestic, time-sensitive reference and is not an FOB or CIF figure. Promotions and stock status change; request a current export quotation with destination and Incoterm instead of converting the domestic tag.

## Pre-payment verification list

1. Choose 1.5T or 2.0T and obtain the matching OEM/CoC data; treat the 2.0T output as 'confirm by VIN' until then.
2. Confirm LHD (Chinese market) — no RHD Boyue L is evidenced in these sources.
3. If the buyer calls it 'Starray', obtain the destination-market OEM brochure and compare — do not assume Chinese spec.
4. Verify emission stage (China 6) and destination fuel/emission compatibility.
5. Check head-unit language, navigation band and connected features for the destination.
6. Reconcile VIN across contract, CoC, invoice and shipping documents.

## Why this matters for an SUV order

Compact SUVs are often ordered in mixed trim batches. The avoidable error is shipping a 1.5T unit against a 2.0T purchase contract, or promising a Starray-market feature from a Chinese build. Locking the SKU and the market scope before deposit removes that dispute.

## FAQ
**Is Boyue L the same as the Geely Starray?**

Starray is an export-market nameplate; regional Starray specs can differ from the Chinese Boyue L, so the two are referenced market-by-market rather than treated as identical.

**How big is the Boyue L?**

Chinese-market reference: 4730×1910×1710 mm, 2785 mm wheelbase, 650 L (up to 1610 L) of cargo space.

**What engines are offered?**

A cross-checked 1.5T (133 kW/290 N·m, 7DCT, 92-RON) and a 2.0T (160 kW/325 N·m) whose figure should be confirmed by VIN.

**Is it AWD?**

The referenced Chinese line is front-wheel drive.

**Does it come in right-hand drive from China?**

No RHD Chinese-market version is evidenced here; confirm separately for RHD destinations.
