## Engine, gearbox, axle: the commercial-vehicle core

- **Engine**: confirm displacement, output, emission stage (China-6 / 6b) and the destination's fuel-sulphur and emission compatibility.
- **Gearbox**: light trucks typically use a manual transmission; confirm gear count and ratios for the load/route.
- **Axle & final drive**: confirm rear-axle ratio against laden operation and terrain.
- **Chassis**: cab-over layout maximises cargo length within a compact footprint; verify wheelbase against the intended 4.2 m-class body.

## Payload and GVW must come from the announcement

## Chassis-to-superstructure discipline

Many J6F exports are a chassis plus a locally built body (box, stake, refrigerated, tanker). Keep two approvals separate: the OEM chassis homologation and the bodybuilder's superstructure compliance, both in the destination. Mismatched chassis-GVW and body weight is a common reason a light truck fails registration or overloads.

## Export and pre-payment checklist

1. Obtain the exact chassis announcement record: engine model/output, GVW, kerb mass, rated payload, wheelbase.
2. Match the cargo-body length and type to the chassis and to destination dimension/axle rules.
3. Confirm emission stage and diesel quality tolerance; plan spares for the engine actually fitted.
4. Chinese market is LHD; steering and any export calibration need separate OEM evidence.
5. For upfitted units, document chassis and superstructure conformity separately.
6. Reconcile VIN/chassis number across contract, CoC, invoice and shipping documents.

## Quotation note

No FOB/CIF figure is estimated. Request a current export quotation naming destination, quantity, chassis configuration, body type, steering, port and Incoterm, and price the upfit separately from the chassis.
