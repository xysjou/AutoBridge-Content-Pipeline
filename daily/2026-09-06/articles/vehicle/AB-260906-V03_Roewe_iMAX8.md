The Roewe iMAX8 is SAIC's mid/large people-carrier — a five-door, seven-seat (2+2+3) front-wheel-drive MPV aimed at family and executive-shuttle use. For export, an MPV is bought for its seating and space first and its horsepower second, so this page leads with cabin architecture. It covers the petrol iMAX8 only; any hybrid or electric iMAX8 variant is a different product and is deliberately not mixed in.

The 3000 mm wheelbase and 2+2+3 arrangement are what make the iMAX8 useful as an airport/hotel shuttle or large-family vehicle: walk-through access to the third row and independent middle-row seats. Buyers should confirm whether the target trim uses captain chairs or a bench and whether the rear cabin includes the rail/console features, since MPV equipment varies materially by SKU.
## Powertrain: one engine family, several year calibrations
The petrol iMAX8 uses a 2.0-litre turbo (20A4E family) paired with an Aisin 8-speed automatic. Independent sources quote different outputs for different model years — roughly 172 kW / 360 N·m in one calibration and 231 PS / 370 N·m in another. These are model-year calibration differences, not a contradiction, and the article does not select a single 'true' figure. The correct action is to bind the output to the exact model-year SKU on the OEM/CoC sheet.
## What MPV buyers should verify beyond power
- Seat count and second-row type (captain chairs vs bench) for the precise trim;
- Sliding-door configuration and climate zones;
- Suspension tune and ride height for road conditions in the destination;
- Interior material and infotainment language for chauffeur/fleet presentation;
- Spare-wheel and luggage space behind the third row, which matters for airport transfer work.
## Scope discipline on variants
This page is limited to the petrol iMAX8. Hybrid and pure-electric iMAX8 derivatives — where they exist in other model years — carry different batteries, weights and service requirements and must be documented on their own pages. Equally, Roewe is the SAIC domestic brand; an MG-badged or other-market MPV is not assumed identical without explicit OEM evidence.
## Chinese price is domestic-only
The Chinese guide price is a time-sensitive domestic reference, not an export quotation; do not convert it to FOB/CIF. Request a current export quote naming destination, quantity, trim, steering and Incoterm.
## Export and pre-payment checklist
1. Fix model year and bind the 2.0T output to that SKU in writing.
2. Confirm the 2+2+3 seat configuration and door type for the actual build.
3. Chinese market is LHD; no RHD iMAX8 is established in these sources — RHD markets need separate evidence.
4. Pull MIIT/CoC data for dimensions, emission stage and seated capacity.
5. Check China-6 calibration against destination fuel/emission rules.
6. Match VIN across all trade and shipping documents before balance payment.
## FAQ
