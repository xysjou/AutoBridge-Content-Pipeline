The HOWO TX is Sinotruk China National Heavy Duty Truck Group's mainstream heavy tipper (dump truck) for construction and muck-hauling, sold in 6×4 and 8×4 drive layouts. Heavy-truck purchasing is about driveline, axles, gross combination weight, cab and chassis — and about recognising that one sample specification does not represent the whole model matrix. HOWO's status as Sinotruk's core heavy-truck brand is confirmed by the company's official disclosures; the detailed working figures come from homologation paper.

## Reading a heavy tipper the way a fleet does
- Engine: displacement, rated power/torque and emission stage; match to destination fuel sulphur and emission regime.
- Gearbox: manual multi-speed (e.g. 12-speed) with the right ratios for laden gradients; confirm PTO provision for hydraulic tipping.
- Axles / suspension: drive-axle ratio and bogie rating set traction and legal load; confirm reduction and tyre specification.
- GCW/GVW and chassis: legal gross and combination weights define productivity; these come from the homologation sheet.
- Cab: cab-over/short-bonnet TX cab, suspension-seat and sleeper options for long-haul versus short-cycle site work.
## Tipper body and hydraulics: separate from the chassis
> Body/bed dimensions, hoist (hydraulic lifting) specification, GVW, kerb mass and rated payload are not asserted as fixed values — they require the MIIT announcement and the bodybuilder specification. The dump body is often fitted or chosen separately, so chassis homologation and tipper-body compliance are two documents, especially for export.
## Export and pre-payment checklist
1. Specify 6×4 or 8×4 and obtain the exact engine/gearbox/axle from the chassis announcement for the VIN.
2. Secure GVW/GCW, axle loads, kerb mass and rated payload; size the tipper body to legal payload, not assumption.
3. Confirm PTO, hydraulic pump and hoist spec and who supplies them (OEM vs bodybuilder).
4. Check China-6 calibration against destination diesel sulphur/emission rules and parts availability.
5. Chinese market is LHD; export/RHD or regional HOWO builds are documented separately and not assumed identical.
6. Plan shipping for a heavy/oversize unit (RoRo vs flat-rack/break-bulk) and reconcile all chassis numbers.
## Quotation discipline
Heavy-truck pricing is configuration- and body-dependent and is not estimated here. Request a current export quotation with destination, quantity, exact driveline, tipper-body spec, steering, port and Incoterm; quote chassis and superstructure separately.
