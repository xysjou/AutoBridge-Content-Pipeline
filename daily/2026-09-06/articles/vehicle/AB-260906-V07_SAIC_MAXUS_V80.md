The Xintu ('New Route') V80 is SAIC MAXUS's large van (big-VAN) family — sold as a passenger minibus, a crew van and a panel/cargo van across several body lengths. Commercial buyers choose it for cargo volume, seating and GVW, so this page is organised around the body matrix and the documents that prove payload. It covers the 2025 V80 PRO with the 2.0T diesel; the older 2.5T is a legacy powertrain and is not carried into this reference.

## Current powertrain (2025 PRO)
- Engine: SAIC π 2.0T diesel, 108 kW (147 PS) / 375 N·m for the 2025 PRO (corroborated).
- Transmission: 6-speed manual or 6-speed AMT (automated manual), by trim.
- The older 2.5T (≈100 kW / 330 N·m) is a legacy line and is explicitly excluded so the two are not mixed on a quotation.
## Payload and GVW: insist on the homologation sheet
> Rated payload, kerb mass and gross vehicle weight are NOT asserted here — they require the MIIT announcement / certificate of conformity for the exact chassis and body. For a cargo van these numbers are the whole purchase case, so obtain them before deposit rather than estimating from the body size.
## How commercial buyers should evaluate the V80
1. Define the mission (passenger shuttle, city delivery, ambulance/camp conversion) and pick body length, roof and seats accordingly.
2. Obtain the exact GVW, rated payload and kerb mass from the homologation/CoC record for that SKU.
3. Choose 6MT vs 6AMT for driver availability and duty cycle; confirm RWD/FWD for the chosen body.
4. Check diesel emission stage and fuel-sulphur tolerance for the destination; confirm service intervals and spares.
5. For conversions (refrigerated, camper, minibus), separate the chassis homologation from the bodybuilder's superstructure approval.
6. Chinese market is LHD; any export/RHD build needs separate OEM evidence; match VIN across all documents.
## Van export considerations
Large vans are frequently upfitted before export. Keep the OEM chassis warranty/homologation distinct from the local bodybuild, confirm whether the destination treats the vehicle as N-category goods or M-category passenger for registration, and request a current export quotation by configuration rather than converting the time-sensitive Chinese domestic price.
## FAQ
What engine does the current V80 use?
The 2025 Xintu V80 PRO uses a SAIC π 2.0T diesel at 108 kW (147 PS)/375 N·m, with 6-speed manual or AMT; the old 2.5T is not covered here.
How many seats does it have?
It depends on the version: passenger versions from 5–9 up to 15 seats, cargo versions 2/3 seats; confirm for the exact body.
What body lengths are offered?
Roughly 4950/5490/5700 mm with 3100/3640/3850 mm wheelbases by version (one independent source matrix to confirm).
What is its payload?
Payload/GVW are not quoted here — they must come from the MIIT announcement/CoC for the specific chassis.
Is it right-hand drive?
The Chinese-market V80 is LHD; an RHD or export build requires separate OEM evidence.
