The Geely Preface (Chinese name Xingrui) is the compact/mid-size front-wheel-drive four-door sedan Geely developed on the CMA (Compact Modular Architecture) shared with wider Geely-group products. For an overseas buyer sourcing from China the key discipline is version control: the line has been sold across several model years with both 1.5-litre and 2.0-litre turbo petrol powertrains, and quoted power figures differ between older and newer trims. This page is a Chinese-market reference; it is not a global specification sheet, and any export-market Preface must be evidenced separately.
## Where the Preface sits in the line-up
- Body: four-door, five-seat three-box sedan, front-engine/front-wheel drive, on the CMA platform.
- Reference dimensions (Chinese market, current Dongfangyao-era line): 4785 × 1869 × 1469 mm with a 2800 mm wheelbase; note an older line listing shows a 4825 mm length for a different model-year body, so match length to the exact year.
- Suspension: MacPherson strut at the front and multi-link independent rear, consistent with the CMA layout.

The two 2.0T outputs are not a data conflict to resolve by picking one: they belong to different model-year calibrations. An export quotation that names 'a Preface 2.0T' without the year and exact trim is ambiguous. Geely's regional Middle-East materials show a separate export-market Preface powertrain (for example a 2.0TD + 7DCT regional build); under model-identity discipline that regional build is cited as an export-market reference and is not treated as identical to the Chinese 175/200 kW trims without OEM confirmation.
## Chassis, seating and equipment signals
The CMA platform gives the Preface an independent rear axle, which distinguishes it from cheaper torsion-beam sedans in the segment. Higher Chinese trims carry the large central screen, panoramic roof and L2 driver-assist features seen on the OEM configuration page. Exact equipment — screen size, driver-assist sensor set, seat functions — is trim-specific and changes by model year, so build the order against the configuration sheet for the actual SKU rather than a generic feature list.
## Chinese-market price is not an export price
The Chinese manufacturer guide price is a domestic reference only. It is time-sensitive, varies by trim and promotion, and must never be converted into an FOB/CIF export quotation. For a landed buying decision use the CTA below and specify destination, quantity, trim and Incoterm.
## Overseas buyer verification before deposit
1. Lock model year and exact trim, then map the engine (1.5T vs which 2.0T calibration) to that SKU in writing.
2. Confirm steering: the Chinese-market car is left-hand drive; no OEM evidence establishes a factory right-hand-drive Preface here, so RHD markets need a separate answer.
3. Pull the MIIT announcement/CoC (certificate of conformity) data for the exact homologated model to confirm dimensions, engine and emission stage.
4. Do not assume the regional 'Preface' sold in a Gulf market equals the Chinese car in power or equipment — request the export-market OEM sheet.
5. Match the VIN on the contract, invoice, B/L and CoC before the balance is paid.
## Export considerations for this sedan
- Emissions/fuel: a Chinese-spec petrol car is calibrated to Chinese fuel and the China-6 stage; destination emission and fuel-grade compatibility must be checked.
- Infotainment/language and connected services: China-market head units may lack the destination language, app store or frequency band — test before order.
- Warranty: a domestic Chinese warranty does not automatically transfer abroad; agree service/spares terms in the contract.

## FAQ
Is the Geely Preface the same as the Xingrui?
Xingrui is the Chinese domestic name and Preface the export/English name for the model line, but Chinese and regional-export trims and powertrains differ — verify the specific build rather than assuming identical specs.
What engine does the Preface use?
The Chinese market offers 1.5T and 2.0T turbo-petrol variants; the 2.0T exists in older 175 kW and newer 200 kW calibrations for different model years, so confirm by exact trim.
Is it front- or all-wheel drive?
The Chinese-market Preface is front-wheel drive across the referenced line.
Is there a right-hand-drive factory version?
No RHD version is established by the Chinese-market sources here; RHD eligibility needs separate OEM evidence.
Can I use the Chinese price as the export price?
No — the Chinese guide price is a time-sensitive domestic reference only; request a formal export quotation with Incoterm and destination.
