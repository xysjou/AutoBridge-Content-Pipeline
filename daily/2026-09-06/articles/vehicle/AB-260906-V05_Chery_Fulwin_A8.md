The Fulwin A8 (Chinese Fengyun A8) is Chery's compact plug-in hybrid sedan built on the Kunpeng C-DM hybrid system — a five-seat, front-wheel-drive PHEV that pairs a petrol engine with an electric motor and a traction battery. PHEVs reward careful reading because two 'range lines' with different batteries and outputs sit under one model name, alongside a longer A8L body. This is a Chinese-market reference that keeps those lines separate.

## The two PHEV lines, read separately
The Fulwin A8 is sold in two distinct battery/range lines that share a body but not a specification. The better-evidenced 145 line and the one independent source 70 line are described separately below so the two are never quoted as one car.
### 145 km line (better evidenced)
- 1.5T engine (156 PS; engine 115 kW / 220 N·m) plus electric motor;
- Battery ≈18.0–18.67 kWh; CLTC all-electric range 145 km;
- System combined ≈265 kW / 530 N·m; combined range ≈1400 km (CLTC-based claim);
- DC fast-charging 30%→80% in about 19 minutes (one independent source — confirm).
### 70 km line (one independent source — confirm before contracting)
- 1.5L engine (102 PS) plus motor; 9.525 kWh battery; CLTC electric range 70 km;
- WLTC combined range ≈1310 km and depleted-battery fuel use ≈4.55 L/100 km;
- These figures currently rest on a single database and should be verified on the OEM sheet before being written into a sale.
The two lines are different cars in battery, engine and system output. A purchase contract must name the line (70 or 145) and the exact SKU; mixing a 70-line battery with a 145-line system-output claim is a typical PHEV quotation error.
## How the C-DM system shapes the buying decision
As a PHEV the Fulwin A8 operates in electric-only, hybrid and battery-depleted (charge-sustaining) modes. For an overseas fleet the relevant questions are: real electric-only distance under local driving, the depleted-battery fuel economy when charging is unavailable, and whether the destination has the plug/charging infrastructure to use the battery at all. Without convenient charging a buyer is effectively carrying a heavier hybrid — size the choice to the use case.

## PHEV export and pre-payment checks
1. Name the range line (70 vs 145) and distinguish A8 from the longer A8L on every document.
2. Bind engine, battery kWh, electric range (CLTC) and system output to the exact SKU; mark one independent source items 'confirm'.
3. Confirm charging hardware (connector, AC/DC) against destination infrastructure.
4. Check China-6 engine calibration, fuel grade and hybrid-mode servicing/spares in the destination.
5. Verify cockpit language and connected features; Chinese market is LHD with no RHD evidenced here.
6. Match VIN across contract, CoC, invoice and B/L; use the correct battery UN classification for shipping.

## FAQ
What is the difference between the 70 and 145 Fulwin A8?
They are distinct PHEV lines: the 145 uses a 1.5T engine and an ≈18 kWh battery for 145 km CLTC electric range and higher combined output; the 70 uses a 1.5L and 9.525 kWh for 70 km, with weaker sourcing.
Is the 1400 km range an electric range?
No — it is a combined (electric+fuel) CLTC-based figure; the all-electric CLTC ranges are 70 or 145 km by line.
Is the Fulwin A8 the same as the A8L?
No — the A8L is a longer-bodied variant (≈4790 mm) and must not share the A8’s specs.
Who supplies the battery?
Battery supplier is not stated as fact here; it must be confirmed per batch by Chery.
Can it fast-charge?
The 145 line is quoted at ≈19 minutes for 30–80% (one independent source, confirm); verify connector and standard for the destination.
