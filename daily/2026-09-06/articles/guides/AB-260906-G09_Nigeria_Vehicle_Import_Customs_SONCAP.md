Nigeria pairs customs clearance with a mandatory pre-shipment conformity regime, and for a Chinese exporter the conformity side is largely completed **before** the goods leave China. The **Nigerian Customs Service (NCS)** handles entry, valuation and duty; the **Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON)** runs SONCAP for regulated products. This is a Nigeria-specific guide; it carefully separates what SONCAP covers from the questions only NCS can answer.

## SONCAP: a two-stage, offshore-then-shipment regime

SON describes SONCAP as an offshore and pre-shipment scheme to control regulated imports. It works in two stages. First, a **Product Certificate (PC)** — PC1 (unregistered), PC2 (registered) or PC3 (licensed) — is obtained for the product through a SON-appointed International Accredited Firm (IAF). Second, per shipment, a **Certificate of Conformity (CoC)** is verified and the **SONCAP Certificate (SC)** issued. SON states these documents are mandatory for importation and customs clearance of SONCAP-regulated goods, with a legal basis that includes SON Act No.14 of 2015 and the pre-shipment inspection framework.

Crucially, the IAF documents feed the importer's **Form M** and the **Pre-Arrival Assessment Report (PAAR)** used to clear with NCS — meaning a missing SONCAP document stalls the customs entry itself, not just a standards check. The Chinese government's own regulatory summary confirms SONCAP's mandatory scope covers automotive items such as tyres, vehicle glass, auto parts and vehicle batteries; a whole-vehicle exporter should confirm with SON/IAF exactly which components on the unit fall within regulated categories.

## The document chain in order

1. Establish whether the product (vehicle and/or its regulated components) is SONCAP-regulated.
2. Obtain the appropriate Product Certificate via a SON-appointed IAF.
3. The Nigerian importer opens Form M; the PC/SONCAP paperwork supports it.
4. Pre-shipment inspection/verification by the IAF produces the CoC and then the SONCAP Certificate.
5. NCS processes PAAR and the entry; duty is assessed on the applicable customs value and tariff.
6. Goods clear only when the conformity and customs documents align.

## NCS: duty, valuation and the questions this guide does not pre-answer

Nigeria Customs applies the Common External Tariff (aligned with ECOWAS) and its valuation/clearance procedures to vehicle imports.

> No fixed duty, VAT, levy or CET rate, no used-vehicle age limit, no list of permitted land-border entry points, and no SONCAP fee/processing time is asserted here. Those require the current NCS tariff/fiscal-policy sources and current SON schedules; without an official primary text they remain items to confirm, not facts to print. SONCAP fees are published by SON and are time-sensitive.

## Where Chinese exporters typically go wrong

- Treating SONCAP as an arrival-time formality — it is built offshore, before shipment;
- Using an unaccredited 'agent' instead of a SON-appointed IAF, so the PC/CoC is not recognised;
- Mismatching product/model on the PC, Form M, invoice and B/L so PAAR cannot reconcile;
- Assuming a whole vehicle is entirely outside SONCAP because its regulated parts (tyres, glass, battery) are separately covered;
- Quoting a fixed duty or age rule without the current NCS source.

## Pre-shipment checklist for the China side

- Confirm regulated status and the correct PC route with a SON-appointed IAF early; align model, HS and VIN/serial across PC, invoice and B/L; obtain CoC/SC before sailing; leave duty and age questions to current NCS/fiscal sources via the importer; keep the IAF appointment evidence in the transaction file.

## FAQ
**What does SONCAP stand for?**

The Standards Organisation of Nigeria Conformity Assessment Programme, a mandatory pre-shipment conformity scheme for regulated imports.

**What are PC, CoC and SC?**

A Product Certificate (PC1/PC2/PC3) is the product stage; the Certificate of Conformity and SONCAP Certificate are the per-shipment stage needed for clearance.

**Does SONCAP cover vehicles?**

It applies to regulated products, including automotive items such as tyres, glass, parts and batteries; confirm the exact scope for the unit with SON/IAF.

**How does SONCAP connect to customs?**

The conformity documents support Form M and the PAAR that Nigeria Customs uses to clear the goods.

**What is the import duty or age limit?**

This guide gives no fixed figure — obtain current NCS tariff/fiscal-policy sources; rates and age policy are time-sensitive.
