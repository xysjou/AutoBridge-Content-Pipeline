## Buyer context: a heavy tipper defined by chassis plus superstructure
The Shacman Delong M3000S (Delong M3000S) dump truck is sold in China-V and China-VI engine lines. A tipper is always a chassis plus a tipping superstructure, so payload and body dimensions are configuration-specific and cannot be quoted as one universal M3000S number. Reference market is China.

## Dump-truck export checks: engine line, axles and the body
Separate China-V from China-VI builds and name the exact Weichai engine. Rated load/GVW, cargo-box size and tipping geometry depend on the ordered superstructure and are withheld rather than estimated; the one independent source kerb figure is a reference for one build only. For Central-Asia/Africa routes, match the emission stage, axle rating and tyre spec to road and loading conditions, and confirm parts support for Weichai/Fast/Hande components.
A tipper is really two procurements in one — a chassis and a separately built body — so the buyer should pin both before payment. On the chassis side, record the exact engine model and rated power, transmission, axle reduction and suspension, because the same M3000S nameplate can be delivered with several drivelines. On the body side, specify steel grade and thickness, side/board height, internal volume, tailgate type, the hydraulic hoist make and cylinder stages, and whether the body is welded to the frame or mounted on a sub-frame. Overloading a body that is lighter than the chassis rating is the most common route to cracked frames and overturned beds on quarry grades. Ask the supplier to state which figures are homologated chassis values and which are upfitter values, and do not merge the two into a single "payload" on the contract.
## Overseas buyer verification before payment
1. Choose WP11 or WP8 and the China-V/China-VI stage explicitly.
2. Specify the tipping body (volume, steel, hoist) and obtain GVW/payload from the exact build.
3. Confirm Fast gearbox model and Hande axle ratings.
4. Verify destination emission/import rules and spare-parts channel.
5. Request a current export quotation; none is published.
