## Buyer context: one MPV nameplate, two energy lines
The Dongfeng Forthing Youting (Ting (yacht)) is a mid-size Chinese-market MPV offered in ICE and PHEV lines that must be quoted separately. They share a 2900 mm wheelbase and 7-seat, hinged-door cabin but differ in height and powertrain. Reference market is China; no export-line specification is claimed.
## Cabin, dimensions and the two lines
- Wheelbase/seating: 2900 mm, 7(5doors, swing door) (five doors, hinged/sliding as built — confirm by trim).
- ICE body: 4850 / 1900 / 1715 mm (corroborated).
- PHEV body: 4850 / 1900 / 1740 mm (corroborated; note the taller roofline).
- PHEV powertrain: 1.5T 154PS L4, engine torque 230 N·m, motor torque 310 N·m, 1-speed DHT, top speed 180 km/h.

## MPV procurement focus: seating, conversion and what is deliberately missing
For MPV importers the order-critical fields are seat count/configuration, door type, cabin length and whether the unit is ICE or PHEV. The PHEV battery capacity and pure-electric range are not reliably captured and are withheld, and fast-charging rests on a one independent source; do not fill these from assumption. Any ambulance, VIP shuttle or wheelchair conversion is an upfit built on this MPV and belongs to a separate special-vehicle assessment.
