## Where the Poer Sits in GWM's Line-up

The GWM Poer (Chinese name 炮 / Pao) is Great Wall's core pickup platform. The reference vehicle here is the **2024 passenger version (乘用版), REFERENCE MARKET: CHINA**; GWM also builds a **commercial version (商用版)** and an **off-road version (越野版)**, plus a box-bodied passenger variant, and their chassis, bed and suspension differ substantially. Overseas the truck is sold as the **GWM Poer / GWM Ute** across the Middle East, Australia/New Zealand and Latin America, in petrol and diesel and in both steering layouts. Because "Poer" covers several very different builds, the version must be fixed before any spec comparison is meaningful.

## Powertrain: Petrol vs Diesel

| Engine | Type | Power / torque | Typical fitment |
|---|---|---|---|
| GW4C20B 2.0T petrol | Turbo, direct injection | 140 kW (190 PS) / 360 N·m | Passenger 2.0T |
| 2.4T diesel | Turbo-diesel | 135 kW (186 PS) / **480 N·m** | Passenger 2.4T diesel |

Both automatic passenger versions use a **ZF 8-speed torque-converter automatic (8AT)**. The diesel gives up almost nothing in power but adds 120 N·m of torque, which is the relevant choice for towing and loaded running. These drivetrain figures are CROSS_CHECKED across Autohome, PCauto and 360che. Manual and alternative transmissions exist on other (commercial) versions and should be specified separately rather than assumed.

## Chassis, Drive and Wheelbase

- **Drive**: the passenger 4×4 uses an **on-demand (part-time/torque-on-demand) four-wheel-drive** system; 4×2 is also offered. Confirm whether the quoted truck is 4×2 or 4×4 and the exact 4WD type (on-demand vs part-time with low range on off-road versions).
- **Wheelbase**: **3230 mm** for the passenger standard-bed; **3470 mm** for the commercial long-bed. Do not mix the two.
- **Body**: 4-door, 5-seat double cab.
- **Overall size (reference)**: 5416 × 1947 × 1886 mm for the box-bodied 2.0T passenger variant — this is a **single-source** dimension, so match it to the exact VIN/version before using it on documents.

## Cargo Bed and Payload — Verify, Don't Assume

For a pickup, the bed and rated payload are the commercial heart of the purchase:

- A passenger-version bed reference of approximately **1520 × 1520 × 1050 mm** is recorded from a single source (PCauto); treat it as indicative and confirm the internal length/width/height on the chosen cab/bed combination — standard vs long bed changes these numbers.
- **Payload capacity, GVW, towing rating, axle ratings and tyre/load index were not captured in the Stage-1 fact base and are deliberately not invented here.** Request these from the official/commercial specification sheet for the exact version and destination market; they vary by suspension, wheelbase and whether the truck is passenger- or commercial-rated.

## Export Verification for Pickup Importers

- Confirm **LHD/RHD** for the destination and that the required engine (petrol/diesel) is produced in that steering layout.
- Verify **emissions/fuel-standard acceptance** — diesel availability and the accepted emission stage differ sharply by country (and by Middle East/GCC vs LatAm vs ANZ requirements); confirm against destination regulation, not the Chinese label.
- Obtain **payload, GVW, towing and axle ratings** on the official sheet before fleet quotations.
- Confirm bed dimensions and whether a bed liner/cover/sport bar is factory or accessory fitment.
- Match warranty terms: a Chinese reference of five years / 200,000 km is single-source and China-market coverage; overseas warranty follows the distributor.

## Buyer FAQ

**Petrol or diesel Poer?** The 2.0T petrol makes 140 kW/360 N·m; the 2.4T diesel makes 135 kW but 480 N·m — choose diesel for load and towing.
**What gearbox does the passenger version use?** A ZF 8-speed automatic; other versions may use different transmissions, so confirm by version.
**How long is the wheelbase?** 3230 mm for the passenger standard bed and 3470 mm for the commercial long bed.
**What is the payload?** It was not in the verified fact base and must be taken from the exact version's official sheet — do not rely on a generic Poer number.
**Is the Poer sold in RHD?** GWM sells Poer/Ute in both steering layouts across many markets, but confirm the specific engine/version is available in RHD for your country.
