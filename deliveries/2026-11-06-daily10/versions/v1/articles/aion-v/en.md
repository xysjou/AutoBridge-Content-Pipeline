# AION V UK: is Premium Pack worth choosing for your cabin?

A softer-looking seat is an easy upgrade to sell. Deciding whether it improves the way you actually use a car takes more care. For the UK AION V, the useful starting distinction is unusually concrete: the published UK specification separates base leatherette upholstery from genuine leather with Premium Pack. That gives a buyer something to compare, but it does not establish that every comfort feature requires the package or that a similarly named car abroad carries the same equipment.

Premium Pack is more than an upholstery choice: the UK specification also assigns front-seat massage, a second-row tray table, a CoolHot Box and a PM2.5 sensor to the package. The strongest reason to choose it is that these particular additions match how your household uses the cabin. The weakest reason is assuming that “Premium” automatically means better space, a different safety result, or an export-ready ownership experience. Those are separate questions. This guide concerns the UK battery-electric SUV and its local package terminology, using sources checked on 6 November 2026. Equipment availability should be reconfirmed against the dated order specification.

## Compare the seats, not the adjective

AION Auto UK's specification page identifies the upholstery difference. It marks heated and ventilated front seats, a heated steering wheel and heated second-row seats as standard. Premium Pack therefore should not be presented as necessary just to obtain those comfort functions. Reconfirm the dated order specification for the actual car.

Ask the seller to mark which comfort functions belong to the quoted base car and which change with the package. A useful comparison has parallel entries for upholstery and each function, rather than a sales description that groups everything under “luxury.” If the seller cannot separate the entries, the upgrade has not yet been explained well enough to assess.

Material preference is personal. Sit in both alternatives if they are available, wearing the sort of clothing you normally drive in. Consider how the surface feels, whether you like its appearance, and whether the seller can provide the applicable care instructions. This is a comparison exercise, not a claim that genuine leather necessarily stays cooler, wears better or needs less attention than leatherette. None of those outcomes is established here.

A household that prefers leatherette should not treat the more expensive-sounding label as an instruction to upgrade. Equally, a buyer who strongly prefers the feel of genuine leather can give that preference real weight. The sensible question is whether the documented difference is one you will appreciate repeatedly, rather than whether the badge sounds more desirable.

## Decide whether you will use the additional functions

The front-seat massage function deserves its own demonstration. Try the controls while safely parked and decide whether the experience appeals to you. Its inclusion is not evidence of a medical benefit or a guarantee that every driver will find it comfortable. If you would rarely switch it on, it should carry little weight in your decision.

For rear passengers, ask to see the second-row tray table opened and stowed with the front seat in its normal position. Consider what you would use it for and whether the arrangement suits the intended passenger. Its presence does not establish extra cabin space or make every use appropriate while the vehicle is moving; follow the vehicle instructions.

The CoolHot Box is described with heating, freezing and refrigeration functions. Ask for a demonstration of selecting those functions and accessing the compartment. No temperature, food-preservation, medical-storage or performance guarantee is implied here. For a particular intended use, obtain the applicable operating instructions rather than assuming it replaces a household appliance.

The PM2.5 sensor is another documented package addition. Ask what information it displays and how that information is presented. A sensor's presence is not evidence of a health benefit or a guarantee about cabin air quality. These separate demonstrations help you value the actual package, instead of paying for an undifferentiated list of attractive terms.

## Comfort includes finding the controls

Seat material is easy to notice in a showroom. Reaching the functions you use every day deserves a separate demonstration. Top Gear's review praises the AION V's space but raises concerns about controls and ride. Auto Express likewise describes a spacious rear cabin and useful storage, while commenting on dependence on the touchscreen. These are the reviewers' assessments, not AutoBridge driving impressions or promises about your experience.

Use those observations to design a focused viewing. With the car safely stationary, ask the seller to show how to adjust the cabin temperature, find the seat-comfort functions and return to the ordinary driving display. Then repeat the actions yourself. If a function you use often feels awkward to reach, a nicer seat covering will not answer that concern.

Keep ride assessment separate too. If a test drive is available, choose a lawful route representative of your normal journeys and judge whether you are comfortable. This article does not say that Premium Pack changes the ride or resolves the reviewers' criticisms. A package name alone supplies no such evidence.

## Give rear passengers a voice

The positive comments about rear space make the AION V worth evaluating with the people who will actually travel in it. They do not establish that every family will find the seating arrangement ideal. Set the front seat for the intended driver before a rear passenger sits down; otherwise the demonstration can flatter the rear compartment without reflecting your use.

Bring the bags or everyday objects that determine your choice, where practical and with the seller's permission. Look at access, where you would place items and whether passengers can reach their belongings comfortably. This can be more useful than choosing between incompatible luggage figures from different publications.

No boot-volume, height, range, battery or charging number is used here. The exact model year, generation and production boundary have not been established for a particular purchase, and some published dimensional and luggage figures conflict. A number should not win a comparison until its vehicle version and measurement basis are clear. If such a figure determines your purchase, request the appropriate manufacturer document for the actual car rather than borrowing a headline figure.

## A UK reference is not a worldwide equipment promise

GAC's UK launch announcement describes the AION V as a battery-electric family SUV and identifies the partnership with Jameel Motors. AION Auto UK's company information describes the UK joint venture and UK-specification production in Guangzhou. That background explains the local offering; it does not make a China-market quotation or another overseas version interchangeable with it.

For an international transaction, ask the seller to identify the original sales market and the specification being offered. A familiar model name and attractive cabin photographs do not establish that the quoted vehicle is the UK configuration discussed here. The term Premium Pack should remain attached to the dated UK equipment information, not become a shorthand for every upper-level AION V.

The same separation matters for support. A UK launch and a UK commercial partner do not prove that a vehicle exported elsewhere has warranty coverage, software support or an accepted service route at its destination. Obtain transaction-specific answers from the relevant provider before treating those benefits as part of the offer.

## Do not buy a safety equivalence from a name

Euro NCAP's assessment identifies a tested AION V Premium with left-hand drive. It reports a stable passenger compartment in the frontal test and a marginal assessment for the driver's chest in the pole test. Both the positive result and the limitation belong to that assessment and its stated vehicle scope.

“Premium” in that test is not, by itself, proof that the UK Premium Pack car is the same tested configuration. Nor does the report establish that every imported AION V inherits its results. A seller relying on the assessment should explain how the offered vehicle relates to the tested version. Upholstery selection and safety applicability are different decisions, even when the marketing terms look similar.

## Choosing without paying for an assumption

Prefer the base specification when its confirmed equipment meets your needs and you prefer, or are content with, leatherette. Consider Premium Pack when genuine leather, massage, the rear tray table, the CoolHot Box or the PM2.5 sensor provides a meaningful benefit for your use and the actual package has been demonstrated. Pause the decision when the seller cannot explain the equipment difference, when the offered market version is unclear, or when essential destination support remains unanswered.

Keep the final comparison narrow: the cabin you prefer, the controls you can use comfortably and the support that applies to your transaction. There is no documented price advantage, resale prediction or universal recommendation here. A well-chosen base car can be a better fit than an unexplained upgrade.

## Questions buyers ask

### Does Premium Pack add every heating and ventilation feature?

No. The UK specification marks front-seat heating and ventilation, steering-wheel heating and second-row seat heating as standard. Premium Pack adds the documented upholstery and other cabin functions described above; reconfirm the actual order schedule.

### Is the UK car identical to one advertised in another market?

That has not been established. Match the original market and actual specification rather than transferring equipment through the model name.

### Can I use the Euro NCAP Premium result to choose the package?

Not from the name alone. The tested left-hand-drive Premium and UK Premium Pack terminology are not automatic equivalents.

### Why are there no range or luggage numbers?

The purchase-specific model year and generation are not established, and conflicting luggage information should not be resolved by guessing. Obtain version-specific documentation if those measurements are decisive.
