# BYD ATTO 2 in Türkiye: Why the Boost and Comfort Names Need Context

Two ATTO 2 offers can use familiar trim names while describing different vehicles. That is the most important point for a buyer who has read European coverage and then receives a Turkish-market specification. A name such as Comfort is not a worldwide technical definition. The useful starting point is the market-specific equipment table, followed by the particular car's documentation.

This article examines the battery-electric ATTO 2 Boost and Comfort shown by BYD Türkiye, using the technical brochure linked from its Turkish model page and that model page checked on 3 November 2026. It does not describe the ATTO 2 DM-i plug-in hybrid, assert equivalence with a Chinese-market model or substitute another European country's trim schedule. The analysis is based on published documents; no first-hand drive or inspection is claimed.

## The Turkish comparison starts with shared powertrain figures

BYD Türkiye lists both Boost and Comfort with a 45.1 kWh LFP Blade Battery, 130 kW maximum power, 7.9-second 0–100 km/h acceleration and 65 kW DC charging. Both have a 312 km combined WLTP figure and a separate 463 km city WLTP figure. The published 30–80% charging time is 28 minutes. These shared numbers mean that, within this Turkish table, choosing Comfort is not choosing a higher-capacity battery. [BYD Türkiye ATTO 2 model page](https://www.bydauto.com.tr/atto2)

That distinction changes the buying conversation. Instead of assuming that the more expensive-sounding trim provides more electric range, compare the equipment differences with the needs of the people using the car. A buyer primarily concerned with charging frequency should not expect a trim label alone to solve that concern. A buyer who values particular cabin or parking features can examine those features directly, without treating them as evidence of a different propulsion system.

## Cabin equipment is where the named versions separate

On the Turkish model page, Boost is listed with a 10.1-inch multimedia screen, six speakers and a rear-view camera. Comfort is listed with a 12.8-inch screen, eight speakers and a panoramic camera. The page also distinguishes heated front seats and a heated steering wheel in the higher equipment specification. These are regional equipment references, not a guarantee that an unidentified vehicle carrying the Comfort name has the same configuration. [Turkish trim comparison](https://www.bydauto.com.tr/atto2)

For a household choosing between the two, it is more useful to rank those differences than to count them. Someone parking frequently in narrow spaces may want a demonstration of the camera views. Another buyer may care more about seat operation or the controls used every day. A larger display is not, on its own, evidence of a clearer interface. Ask to operate the functions that will actually be used, ideally in the language intended for ownership. That practical exercise can make the equipment comparison more decisive than a collection of feature icons.

The same principle applies to photographs in a sales listing. A photographed screen or camera view can help confirm an equipment claim, but a picture taken from a promotional page is not proof of the offered car. Ask for images tied to the actual stock unit and compare them with the equipment schedule. Where the offer says only “full option,” request a precise description instead of supplying the missing meaning yourself.

## A compact footprint still needs a real loading assessment

The linked Turkish technical brochure lists a length of 4,310 mm, width of 1,830 mm, width with mirrors extended of 2,060 mm and height of 1,675 mm. It states 400 litres of luggage space and 1,340 litres with the seats folded, measured to the roof. The same brochure lists an 11 kW onboard charger and explains that the cable for the vehicle's external energy-sharing function is separately obtained rather than standard equipment. [BYD Türkiye linked technical brochure](https://bydauto.mncdn.com/storage/pdf/byd-atto2-teknik-brosur-mart-2026.pdf)

The brochure’s equipment note refers to 11 March 2026, while its final page shows an issue date of 11 March 2025. That internal date discrepancy is unresolved; it should not be used to establish a particular model year. The current Turkish model page independently supports the principal battery, range and trim comparisons above.

These details support several useful checks. For parking, use the appropriate width measurement rather than confusing body width with the space occupied by the mirrors. For luggage, distinguish the normal seating arrangement from the folded-seat figure. A family carrying passengers cannot automatically use a cargo figure that depends on removing their seating space. Test the actual cases, pushchair or work equipment that determine suitability. The objective is not to disprove a published volume but to establish whether its shape and seat configuration work for the intended load.

The separately supplied energy-sharing cable is also a useful reminder that a function and an included accessory are different things. If external power is important, ask for the correct accessory and its operating instructions in the offer. The feature should not be described as a household backup system or assigned an output figure that this article has not verified. BYD's Turkish explanation is a general description of vehicle-to-load use, not evidence of every possible connected appliance's compatibility. ## Keep the two range figures attached to their labels

The city figure and the combined figure answer different questions within the named test procedure. Presenting the larger city number as the car's unqualified range would obscure the distinction. A clear comparison retains “city WLTP” or “combined WLTP” every time the number appears, including in translated text, image captions and a seller's comparison sheet.

Neither figure supplies an exact answer for an individual motorway journey. A buyer should build the decision around the route, charging opportunities and a reserve appropriate to their circumstances, rather than apply an invented conversion factor. If a long trip is essential, seek relevant evidence for that use and the exact vehicle version. A compact electric car can be assessed honestly without either overstating its reach or dismissing it merely because another model has a larger battery.

Charging comparisons need the same discipline. The quoted interval starts at 30%, not 10%, and ends at 80%, not full. A competing car's time is not directly comparable if its start and finish points differ. Keep the manufacturer's conditions available and ask about the actual charger intended for use. The brochure establishes a specification reference, not a reservation at a functioning public charging point.

## A useful offer identifies more than the badge

For this ATTO 2 reference, the most helpful supplier response would identify original market, production details, battery-electric powertrain and Turkish Boost or Comfort equipment. It would separate the vehicle specification from current price, commercial availability and destination-specific matters. This article does not establish export eligibility, approval for another country, transferable warranty coverage or connected-service operation after a cross-border sale.

**Does Comfort always mean a bigger ATTO 2 battery?** No. The Turkish specification examined here gives both named versions the same battery capacity. Do not import a different country's definition of the trim.

**Can the 463 km city figure replace the combined figure in an advertisement?** It should remain explicitly labelled city WLTP. Removing that label changes what the reader understands.

**Is ATTO 2 DM-i covered here?** No. This article is limited to the specified battery-electric versions. Plug-in-hybrid information belongs in a separate, correctly identified comparison.

For a buyer considering these Turkish-market versions, the choice is principally about equipment within the documented shared powertrain specification. Establish that identity first, then decide which cabin and parking features are worth paying for in the actual offer.
