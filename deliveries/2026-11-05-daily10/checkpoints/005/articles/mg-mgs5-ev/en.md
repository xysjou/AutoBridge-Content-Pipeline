# MGS5 EV: choosing UK SE or Trophy from the October 2025 brochure

A battery badge does not settle the MGS5 EV buying decision. The UK October 2025 brochure lists two versions with a nominal 64 kWh battery, but their quoted WLTP range and convenience equipment differ. SE Long Range is the range-led choice within this comparison; Trophy Long Range is the equipment-led alternative. SE Standard Range introduces a smaller nominal battery and a different chemistry. A useful enquiry therefore names the complete trim, rather than asking only for “the 64 kWh MGS5”.

This is a comparison of a dated UK specification, not a declaration of a formal 2026 model year or the specification of stock available today. It concerns the brochure's launch-series, rear-wheel-drive battery-electric five-door SUV. An overseas offer still needs its own vehicle identity, equipment and destination checks. A similar model name or battery figure does not establish that another market's vehicle is identical.

## Three trims, two different reasons to upgrade

All figures below come from the [MG Motor UK October 2025 brochure](https://b.cogcms.co.uk/media/txzcpetj/mgs5_ev_brochure_oct_2025.pdf). Scope: UK, October 2025 edition, launch-series MGS5 EV, BEV and rear-wheel drive throughout. The edition date is not a verified model-year code. WLTP applies only to the combined-range row; other rows are technical specifications without a driving test cycle.

| Parameter | SE Standard Range | SE Long Range | Trophy Long Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nominal battery capacity | 49 kWh | 64 kWh | 64 kWh |
| Battery chemistry | LFP | NCM | NCM |
| Combined range, WLTP | 340 km | 480 km | 465 km |
| Maximum motor power | 125 kW | 170 kW | 170 kW |
| Wheel diameter | 17 inches | 17 inches | 18 inches |

SE Standard Range deserves consideration when a buyer's own route and charging plan do not justify prioritising the larger battery. That is a question to answer with actual travel needs, rather than an assumed universal daily distance. Its smaller nominal capacity is not, by itself, proof of lower running costs, longer battery life or a particular charging time. Those conclusions would need separate evidence.

SE Long Range has the highest combined WLTP figure in this three-trim brochure comparison. It is the natural starting point for someone whose priority is the quoted range rather than additional convenience equipment. Trophy Long Range retains the nominal 64 kWh battery and 170 kW maximum power, yet its listed range is 465 km WLTP. A higher trim name consequently does not mean the longest stated range. None of these laboratory figures is a promise for a particular motorway trip, climate or load.

## Decide which equipment you will actually use

The brochure lists a reversing camera, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, and a 12.8-inch central display for the SE versions. Trophy's listed convenience features include a 360-degree parking camera, heated front seats and steering wheel, a wireless phone charger and a smart electric tailgate. These are concrete reasons to compare Trophy with SE Long Range; “better specification” is too vague to explain a price difference.

For a buyer who parks in a confined space, the camera system may be a useful demonstration request. Someone regularly loading bags may instead want to see the tailgate operate and assess access. Those are purchasing suggestions, not claims that AutoBridge has tested the features. Ask the seller to identify the equipment on the actual car, with photographs or a demonstration linked to its VIN. A stock image showing a Trophy interior cannot establish the specification of a quoted SE.

Keep optional priorities separate from essential requirements. Write down which features would change your decision, which are merely desirable and which do not matter. This makes it easier to compare two offers without treating every advertised feature as equally valuable. It also gives the seller a precise question when the trim description and the photographs appear inconsistent.

## Check the space, not just the litre figure

Across these UK brochure versions, length is 4476 mm, width excluding mirrors is 1849 mm and wheelbase is 2730 mm. The luggage figures are 453 litres with the seats up and 1441 litres folded; the 453-litre figure includes underfloor storage. These measurements are useful starting points for a parking or luggage enquiry, not a guarantee that a particular object will fit.

Measure the intended parking space and the objects you expect to carry. Because the published width excludes mirrors, do not use it alone to decide whether a narrow entrance is suitable. Likewise, a total luggage volume does not reveal the shape of the opening or the dimensions of every compartment. Ask for relevant opening and floor measurements instead of translating litres into an invented number of suitcases. Luggage volume is also not a payload rating.

## Charging details worth resolving before a deposit

The UK brochure identifies a combined Type 2 and CCS charging connection. Connector identification is useful, but it does not answer every charging question. Published descriptions differ on AC charging capability and usable battery capacity, so this comparison does not assign a firm AC maximum or usable-capacity figure. The nominal battery values in the table remain distinct from usable capacity.

Request the manufacturer's charging information for the offered VIN and its precise edition, then discuss compatibility with the proposed charging installation. Ask separately about the supplied cable and the intended charging locations. Do not turn a connector label into a guaranteed charging speed or a full-charge time. If overnight charging is central to the purchase and the seller cannot clarify the car's charging specification, postponing the deposit is a reasonable buying precaution.

## Read the safety result in its proper context

The MGS5 EV received five stars in the [2025 Euro NCAP assessment](https://www.euroncap.com/assessments/mg/mgs5%2Bev/1109/). The assessment also records a frontal-offset penalty associated with driver-side dashboard structures. Reading the result and its limitations together is more informative than quoting the stars alone.

This is a European assessment of the covered vehicle specification. It is not import approval, proof that every other-market MGS5 has identical equipment, or a risk-free driving guarantee. When an overseas seller relies on the rating, ask how the offered vehicle's specification relates to the assessment rather than assuming the badge resolves that question.

## Questions buyers ask

### Does 64 kWh mean Trophy?

No. Both SE Long Range and Trophy Long Range have a nominal 64 kWh battery in the UK October 2025 brochure. Ask for the complete trim and an itemised equipment description.

### Which version has the highest quoted range?

SE Long Range, at 480 km combined WLTP in this brochure, compared with Trophy Long Range at 465 km and SE Standard Range at 340 km. These are laboratory comparisons, not route guarantees.

### Can this comparison establish an overseas warranty or registration outcome?

No. Ask the responsible seller and relevant destination organisations to confirm the individual vehicle's eligibility and support. Current availability, price, connected services and warranty applicability need fresh confirmation; the brochure alone does not settle them.

### What should the enquiry contain?

Name the exact trim and reference edition, explain whether range or convenience equipment is the priority, and request VIN-linked equipment and charging documents. Resolve any mismatch before committing money.
