## Pertanyaan Yang Sering Muncul
**How many range versions are there?** Three documented CLTC steps — 203, 333 and 410 km — with different motor and battery specifications.
**Is every Bingo 50 kW?** No; the 203 km entry car uses a 30 kW motor, while 333/410 versions use 50 kW.
Berapa kursi? Tata letak entri terdokumentasi adalah empat kursi (2+2); mengkonfirmasi apakah kursi versi terpilih lima.
**What charging does it support?** All versions list DC fast charging (about 35 min 30–80%, per Chinese coverage); tipe konektor luar negeri harus diperiksa.
