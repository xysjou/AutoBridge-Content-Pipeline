## Pertanyaan Yang Sering Muncul
**Does the L6's 1.5T engine drive the wheels?** No — it is a generator (range extender); two electric motors (300 kW combined) provide all-wheel drive.
**How far can the L6 go on battery alone?** 212 km CLTC (182 km WLTC) for the 2024 36.8 kWh car; Selalu mengutip siklus.
**What is the combined range?** About 1160 km WLTC with the extender running (cross-checked figure).
**Is the newer L6 the same specification?** No — a later generation uses a ~51 kWh pack and ~300 km CLTC EV range; menjaga tahun model terpisah.
