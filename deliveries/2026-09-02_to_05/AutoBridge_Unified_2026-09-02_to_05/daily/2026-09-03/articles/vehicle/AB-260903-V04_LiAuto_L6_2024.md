## Buyer Context: Read an EREV as Four Systems, Not One Engine
The L6 is a **range-extended electric (EREV) mid-large SUV**: the wheels are driven by electric motors, while a 1.5T petrol engine acts as a generator and does **not** drive the wheels directly. For an EREV the correct reading order is extender → motors → battery → charging → operating mode. This page covers the **2024 Chinese-market L6 (REFERENCE MARKET: CHINA)**; a newer-generation L6 with a larger battery entered China later and is kept separate below.

Because the extender never mechanically drives the wheels, describing the L6 as a conventional 1.5T hybrid is technically wrong and confuses buyers about maintenance (the extender runs to generate charge, not to propel the car at cruising speed).

## Range Modes and Cycles (keep them labelled)
- **All-electric (battery):** 212 km **CLTC** (182 km **WLTC**) — note the two cycles are not interchangeable.
- **Combined (battery + extender):** about 1160 km **WLTC** (cross-checked; label the cycle).
- Operating modes switch between battery-priority and extender-sustained running; explain to buyers that real EV-only distance depends on load, climate and speed, and that the extender needs normal petrol-engine servicing even though it never drives the wheels.

## Charging and Software (EREV-specific checks)
- Chinese builds use the **GB/T** charge interface; confirm destination inlet/protocol on the VIN (adapter ≠ guaranteed handshake).
- Verify **AC/DC charging provision, charge time and any external-discharge (V2L)** feature by grade.
- Li Auto's cockpit/ADAS stack and **OTA/server connectivity** are China-market oriented; confirm UI language, map/account region and whether OTA functions outside China before promising them.

## What Importers Should Verify Before Payment
1. **Model year/generation** (2024 36.8 kWh vs newer 51 kWh) matched to VIN.
2. **Extender fuel/emission** status in the destination **and** the EV-side classification (an EREV still carries a traction battery — shipping follows the EV dangerous-goods route; under IMDG Amendment 42-24 (mandatory from 2026-01-01) a lithium-ion-battery-powered vehicle is classified UN3556, not the legacy generic UN3171; apply documentation/SOC details per the current EV shipping Fact Sheet and the carrier (see the EV ocean-compliance guide)).
3. **Charge connector/protocol**, AC/DC and V2L for the exact grade.
4. **Cockpit language, ADAS and OTA** tested on the VIN outside China.
5. **Pro vs Max** equipment, LHD/RHD availability and warranty coverage.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Does the L6's 1.5T engine drive the wheels?** No — it is a generator (range extender); two electric motors (300 kW combined) provide all-wheel drive.
**How far can the L6 go on battery alone?** 212 km CLTC (182 km WLTC) for the 2024 36.8 kWh car; always quote the cycle.
**What is the combined range?** About 1160 km WLTC with the extender running (cross-checked figure).
**Is the newer L6 the same specification?** No — a later generation uses a ~51 kWh pack and ~300 km CLTC EV range; keep model years separate.
**Does an EREV ship as a dangerous good like a pure EV?** It carries a traction battery, so follow the UN38.3/MSDS ocean dangerous-goods route; under IMDG Amendment 42-24 (mandatory from 2026-01-01) a lithium-ion-battery-powered vehicle is classified UN3556, not the legacy generic UN3171; apply documentation/SOC details per the current EV shipping Fact Sheet and the carrier (see the EV ocean-compliance guide).
