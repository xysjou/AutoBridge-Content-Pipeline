The Changan Kaicene F70 is a body-on-frame (non-load-bearing) mid-size pickup offered as a double cab in two- and four-wheel drive, with standard and long-wheelbase/bed versions including a long-bed "Blue Whale" variant. For a pickup buyer the decisions are physical rather than cosmetic: **bed length, wheelbase, drivetrain and chassis**, and the load/towing numbers that decide whether the truck does the job. This page is a **Chinese-market reference**. It fixes the dimensions that are cross-checked, names the engine families without pinning single-source outputs, and deliberately **withholds** the widely-circulated towing and payload figures because no OEM same-trim sheet or homologation bulletin was captured to support them.

## Body identity and the two wheelbase/bed configurations

- Chassis: **body-on-frame** mid-size pickup, double cab, available 2WD/4WD (cross-checked).
- **Standard-wheelbase reference:** 5330 × 1930 × 1835 mm, wheelbase 3180 mm, cargo bed **1600 × 1595 × 500 mm**.
- **Long-wheelbase / Blue Whale long-bed reference:** 5580 × 1930 × 1835 mm, wheelbase 3430 mm, long bed **1850 × 1595 × 500 mm**.

The 250 mm wheelbase stretch is essentially bed length, and it changes both usable cargo space and turning circle/garage footprint. An order must name standard or long bed explicitly; "an F70" without that designation is ambiguous. Confirm the bed dimensions against the exact SKU's homologation certificate, since accessory liners and bed styling vary by trim.

## Off-road geometry — media figures to confirm

Independent media quote an approach angle of **29°**, departure angle of **27°**, minimum ground clearance of **230 mm** and a maximum wading depth of **900 mm**. These are useful indicators of a genuinely capable ladder-frame pickup, but they are single-source media figures and must be confirmed against OEM data before being written into a tender or off-road commitment. Tyre size, suspension tune and whether the figure is for the 4WD model all matter.

## Towing and payload: deliberately not stated

Circulating media claims of roughly **3.5 t towing** and roughly **1 t payload** are **unverified** in this file — there is no homologation bulletin or OEM same-trim source behind them, and they are therefore **not** presented as facts. For a working pickup these are the most commercially important numbers, so the correct action is to obtain them from the exact model's certificate of conformity / MIIT entry, where rated payload, gross vehicle mass and any approved towing figure are formally recorded. Do not size a trailer or a load against a forum number.

## Steering, fuel and export boundary

The referenced Chinese-market F70 is **left-hand drive**. Any right-hand-drive or export-market Kaicene pickup, and any overseas model name, requires separate OEM evidence and is not assumed identical; a related regional truck is not the same build without an OEM statement. A Chinese-spec diesel is calibrated to local fuel and the China emission stage — verify destination fuel grade and emission compatibility, and check whether the destination accepts the exact engine code.

## FAQ
**What bed lengths does the Kaicene F70 offer?**

A standard 1600 mm bed on a 3180 mm wheelbase and a long/Blue Whale 1850 mm bed on a 3430 mm wheelbase (overall 5330 vs 5580 mm).

**Is it four-wheel drive?**

It is a body-on-frame double-cab pickup offered in both 2WD and 4WD; specify which on the order.

**What are the engine outputs?**

Families include the 2.5T JE4D25 diesel, a 1.9T diesel and a 2.4T petrol with 6MT/6AT, but exact outputs are single-source and intentionally not fixed here — confirm by OEM/MIIT for the exact trim.

**Can it really tow 3.5 tonnes and carry 1 tonne?**

Those circulating figures are unverified in this file; obtain rated towing, payload and GVW from the homologation certificate rather than relying on media claims.

**Is there a right-hand-drive export version identical to this?**

The Chinese truck is LHD; any RHD/export build and overseas naming require separate OEM evidence and are not assumed identical.
