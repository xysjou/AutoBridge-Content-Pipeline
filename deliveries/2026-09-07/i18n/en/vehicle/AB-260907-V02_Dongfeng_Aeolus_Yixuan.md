The Dongfeng Aeolus Yixuan (风神奕炫) is the compact three-box/fastback sedan built on the CMP platform and sold in China under Dongfeng's Aeolus (Fengshen) passenger-vehicle brand. The buyer-risk in this model is temporal rather than structural: the current 2025 on-sale line is a naturally-aspirated 1.5-litre car, while the higher-output "Mach" (马赫) 1.5T belongs to an earlier 2022/2023 powertrain wave. Quoting the older turbo output as if it were the current sedan is the most common version error. This page is a **Chinese-market reference**, not a global spec sheet; export-market versions need separate OEM evidence, and single-source outputs are flagged for confirmation.

## Body identity and dimensions

- Body: four-door, five-seat compact three-box/fastback sedan on the CMP platform, front-engine/front-wheel drive (cross-checked).
- Reference dimensions: **4670 × 1812 × 1490 mm, wheelbase 2680 mm**. Note the original 2019 launch car listed a 4660 mm length; the small year-over-year difference should be matched to the exact model-year certificate rather than blended.

The CMP basis signals a torsion-beam/transverse-front-drive layout aimed at the volume compact-sedan segment; exact suspension and equipment detail for a given SKU should still be read from that SKU's configuration sheet.
