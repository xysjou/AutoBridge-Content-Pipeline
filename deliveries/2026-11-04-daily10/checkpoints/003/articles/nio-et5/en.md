# NIO ET5 Buying Guide: Battery Ownership and the 2023 German Sedan

A NIO ET5 quotation is incomplete until it explains who owns the traction battery. A car offered with a battery purchase and one tied to Battery-as-a-Service can look similar in photographs while creating very different obligations for the next owner. For an overseas buyer, the useful starting point is the battery arrangement, followed by the exact sedan configuration and the support available where the car will be used.

This guide uses the ET5 sedan introduced in Germany in April 2023 as its vehicle reference. It does not describe the ET5 Touring, a China-market car, or a guaranteed specification for every subsequent production year. “2023 launch” identifies the dated German specification, rather than inventing a formal model-year designation.

The relevant market is Germany; the body is the sedan; the battery variants are the launch Standard and Long Range configurations. These values come from [NIO's German launch announcement](https://www.nio.com/de_DE/news/202305150001). They are a reference for checking a quotation, not permission to assign German specifications to an unidentified export vehicle.

Ask the seller for the registration record, VIN, original configuration documentation and photographs of the actual car. Match the body, battery description and optional equipment separately. A registration year alone does not settle the production specification, and a listing that says only “ET5 full option” does not identify what is included.

## Treat the car and battery as two purchasing questions

NIO's German purchase information distinguishes battery rental through BaaS from conventional charging and links Power Swap access to a BaaS subscription in its FAQ. That makes the battery contract a practical part of evaluating the car, rather than an accessory to discuss after agreeing a price. See [NIO's German purchase information](https://www.nio.com/de_DE/get-your-nio/kauf).

For a battery-owned offer, request documentary evidence that the battery is included in the transaction and that the seller can transfer it. For a BaaS-linked offer, ask who the current contracting party is, whether the proposed buyer can enter or take over the relevant arrangement, and what happens when the vehicle moves to the intended destination. Obtain the answer from the responsible provider, not only the trader advertising the car.

A useful quotation comparison has separate lines for the vehicle, battery purchase or rental arrangement, recurring charges, and any proposed transfer or termination costs. Leave an unresolved line unresolved rather than treating it as zero. This is a recommended purchasing method, not a statement that every buyer faces the same fees or contractual route.

The decisive question is simple: after payment and handover, will the buyer have a documented right to use the installed battery under the planned ownership and location? If that answer is unclear, the attractive headline vehicle price is not yet a usable comparison.

## Choose range for the route, not the largest badge

The larger battery offers a higher manufacturer-stated WLTP maximum in the German launch reference. That does not establish a guaranteed daily distance, a particular charging stop, or the remaining capacity of a used pack.

There is also separate test evidence. In its September 2023 test of the German ET5 with the 100 kWh battery, ADAC reported 530 km in its Ecotest and consumption of 19.7 kWh/100 km including charging losses. Those are [ADAC's test results](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/autokatalog/marken-modelle/nio/et5/1generation/326778/), not WLTP values and not an AutoBridge road test. They should remain separate from the manufacturer's range figures.

For a fleet, write down the longest routine trip, available charging locations and consequences of a missed charging opportunity. Ask for a demonstration of the specific car on the equipment the buyer expects to use. A short charging demonstration can establish that a connection and session work; it cannot establish an entire year's operating range or every charging condition.

For a used car, request a dated battery assessment with the test method identified. Compare the assessment with the pack supplied at handover. If any battery change is proposed between inspection and collection, ask for the replacement pack's identity and documentation. Make the delivered configuration auditable rather than assuming every battery history is identical.

## A sedan inspection should include people and luggage

The ET5's dimensions help screen parking and transport suitability, but the buyer's own luggage and passenger requirements deserve a physical check. Try the intended cases through the boot opening, test the rear seating position with the normal front-seat setting, and verify that planned child seats fit the actual car.

Do not substitute ET5 Touring photographs or luggage figures for the sedan. Ask for an uninterrupted walk-around if an in-person inspection is not possible. Include the boot opening, rear bench, charging inlet, wheels and dashboard warnings, rather than accepting only exterior beauty shots.

Safety evidence also has a defined test vehicle. Euro NCAP's 2023 assessment identifies an ET5 sedan with a 75 kWh battery, 19-inch wheels and left-hand drive. It reported a stable passenger compartment in the frontal-offset test, but the child-presence detection system did not meet the requirements for credit. Read the [Euro NCAP assessment](https://www.euroncap.com/assessments/nio/et5/1024/) for complete findings. Neither observation proves the condition of a damaged or repaired car offered today.

## Check support before assigning value to it

NIO's German service page gives an ET5-specific base warranty of three years or 90,000 km, with an extension option, and directs readers to the warranty handbook. Its broader warranty headline is not a substitute for ET5 terms. The [German service information](https://www.nio.com/de_DE/nio-service) does not by itself establish coverage after an independent cross-border sale.

Request confirmation using the actual VIN, first-registration date, destination and ownership arrangement. Identify who would accept a repair request, which documents they need and whether the proposed vehicle remains eligible. Keep any seller warranty separate from the manufacturer's warranty so responsibility is not ambiguous.

NIO also advertises pre-delivery maintenance, a BaaS option and software updates within its [German Certified used-car programme](https://www.nio.com/de_DE/nio-certified-gebrauchtwagen). These are not automatic benefits of every ET5 sold by an independent trader. Ask whether the offered vehicle is actually being sold through that programme and obtain the applicable terms.

## Questions buyers frequently ask

### Does buying an ET5 always mean buying its battery?

No assumption is safe from the model name alone. Request an explicit statement of whether the transaction includes battery ownership or depends on a separate rental arrangement, together with supporting documents.

### Can a German ET5 automatically use swap stations after export?

This guide does not establish that. Confirm eligibility, destination, account and contract with the provider before assigning value to swap access.

### Is the ADAC test distance the car's WLTP range?

No. ADAC Ecotest results and manufacturer-stated WLTP figures are different evidence and should retain their labels. Neither promises performance for a particular used vehicle and route.

### When should a buyer pause the transaction?

Pause if battery ownership or contractual status is unclear, the quoted vehicle cannot be matched to its documents, or promised support depends on an unconfirmed transfer. Resolve these questions before treating prices as comparable.
