# smart #1 Pro+ or Premium? A UK Launch-Spec Buying Guide

The useful distinction between a smart #1 Pro+ and Premium is not simply which advert lists more equipment. It is whether the offered car has the features the buyer will actually use, and whether the charging location can support the capability being advertised. A higher trim can be worthwhile without being the right choice for every household or fleet.

This comparison uses smart's UK launch announcement of 25 April 2023. That is a dated reference for Pro+ and Premium, not a claim that all cars registered in 2023 share identical equipment. It is also not a specification for a China-market vehicle or every later #1. Match the original sales market and vehicle documents before using the comparison to evaluate a quotation.

A dash does not mean absent. It means that the dated announcement is not being used to invent a Pro+ specification by deduction. If the seller advertises a particular onboard charger or heat pump, request the vehicle-specific equipment record and demonstrate the feature where practical.

A later specification page is not an automatic substitute. The [current UK table](https://uk.smart.com/en/models/hashtag-one/specifications/) separately lists Pure, Pro, Pro+, Premium and BRABUS. Those names should not be collapsed into one generic #1 specification, and current values should not silently replace the dated launch reference.

## Decide whether the AC charger matters at your parking place

For a buyer considering Premium, the charging question starts at the parking space. Ask the site operator or installer what power the connection can deliver, how it is shared, and whether the electrical supply and equipment support the advertised capability. Buying the car does not upgrade the electrical installation.

Energy Saving Trust explains that charging duration depends on the vehicle and equipment in its [charging guidance](https://energysavingtrust.org.uk/advice/charging-electric-vehicles/). Its general timing tables are not a charging promise for the smart #1. Nor should an AC onboard-charger rating be substituted for a DC rapid-charging figure.

Consider two purchasing situations. A driver with predictable overnight parking may place greater value on price and condition than on reaching the highest AC rating. A car regularly returning to a suitable workplace charger between appointments may have a different priority. These are decision scenarios, not claims that a battery will replenish in a specified time.

Ask for a charging demonstration with the supplied cable and record the charger, starting battery level and session information. If the result is lower than the advertised peak, investigate the installation and conditions before concluding that the car is faulty. Conversely, a photograph of a successful session does not prove that the hardware matches every claim in the advert.

For a multi-car purchase, compare the cars individually. A fleet listing grouped under one trim name does not establish that every vehicle has the same equipment, cables or software configuration. A vehicle-by-vehicle schedule is more useful than a generic brochure attached to the whole order.

## Make the cabin test about your own passengers

The sliding rear seat makes it particularly important to evaluate luggage space and passenger space together. Try the regular luggage with the rear bench positioned where passengers will sit. A demonstration that maximises the boot by moving the bench forward may not represent the buyer's intended arrangement.

Bring the child seat, pushchair, mobility aid or work case that determines the purchase. Check the opening and loading height as well as whether the item fits. This guide deliberately avoids assigning a family-wide maximum luggage figure to a particular trim or seating position.

Premium's additional equipment should be tried rather than merely counted. View the head-up display from the intended driver's position, listen to audio at a normal volume, and ask the seller to demonstrate fitted parking functions in an appropriate setting. A feature that is difficult for the regular driver to use may have less value than its place on a specification list suggests.

The RAC review criticised reliance on the touchscreen for climate and mirror controls. That is the [reviewer's assessment](https://www.rac.co.uk/drive/car-reviews/smart/1/1/), not an AutoBridge driving impression. It suggests an inspection task: while safely parked, ask the prospective driver to adjust mirrors, temperature and navigation without coaching. Judge the offered car's interface and software rather than assuming every review describes its present state.

## Safety evidence and used-car condition answer different questions

Euro NCAP published its #1 assessment in 2022. Its variant table identifies Premium 4x2 as the tested variant and lists applicability for left- and right-hand-drive versions. It reported a stable passenger compartment in the frontal-offset test, while camera-based traffic-sign recognition did not satisfy requirements for credit; the speed limiter itself was rewarded. Read the [complete assessment](https://www.euroncap.com/assessments/smart/1/0923/) rather than relying on a badge alone.

These findings do not establish whether an individual used vehicle has damage, correct replacement parts or functioning assistance systems. Request repair invoices and a competent inspection appropriate to its history. Warning lights, mismatched tyres, unexplained repairs or missing documents deserve their own answers even when the model has published safety-test results.

For a UK-history car, the [official MOT-history service](https://www.gov.uk/check-mot-history) allows a registration-number lookup of recorded test outcomes and mileage. Compare any available record with the seller's documents and current odometer. A missing record is a question to resolve, not evidence by itself of a clean history or wrongdoing. Do not treat an MOT lookup as a battery-health report, proof of ownership, or approval for registration elsewhere.

## Build a quotation that can be compared

Ask sellers to identify the original sales market, VIN, trim, first-registration date, included cables, equipment record and known repairs. Request a separate description of any offered warranty or service arrangement, including the responsible party and applicable location. Historic launch benefits do not substitute for the terms attached to a used sale.

Compare price only after these items are aligned. A Premium with unresolved equipment or condition questions is not necessarily preferable to a documented Pro+. Equally, a cheaper offer is not better value if the feature motivating the purchase was never established.

For an overseas destination, confirm steering suitability, charging compatibility, service access and the vehicle's acceptance route independently. A UK specification is a reference for the car, not a universal import permission.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Premium always the better choice?

No. Its additional equipment matters only if present, usable and valuable for the buyer's routine. Compare actual condition and documented configuration before assigning value to the badge.

### Does a higher AC rating guarantee faster home charging?

No. The installation, chargepoint and vehicle work together. Ask an installer to evaluate the site rather than assuming the advertised capability is available there.

### Can current Pro+ specifications be applied to an early UK car?

Not without matching evidence. Obtain the equipment record for the vehicle and keep the original market and dated specification together.

### Which unresolved issue should stop a deposit?

Pause if the seller cannot identify the vehicle or substantiate an essential feature, or if the intended use depends on unconfirmed charging or service arrangements. Clarify these before making price the deciding factor.
