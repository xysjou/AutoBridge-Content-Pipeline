# Towbar and Trailer Ratings: Evidence to Request Before Buying

A towbar in a seller's photograph proves that hardware is present. It does not establish how much that particular car can tow, whether the installation matches the vehicle, or whether the intended trailer's brakes and lights will work correctly. For an overseas buyer, those are separate purchasing questions, and each needs a different piece of evidence.

Start with the trailer task, not the largest number in an advertisement. Describe the trailer, its intended loaded condition, the passengers and equipment carried in the tow vehicle, and the destination of use. Then ask the seller to demonstrate that the offered configuration fits that task. The document and acceptance steps below are AutoBridge's recommended procurement method. The UK and New South Wales examples illustrate specific authorities' guidance; they are not worldwide towing rules.

## Read the vehicle's limits before the towbar's label

The vehicle's towing limit and the towbar's own rating are not interchangeable. Volvo's UK manual for the 2026 XC90 explicitly warns that a towbar may be rated for more than the car can tow. It also notes that national rules may impose lower limits. A high number stamped on a coupling cannot therefore settle the purchase decision. [Volvo XC90 towing guidance](https://www.volvocars.com/uk/support/car/xc90/25w17/article/47d2c97fd33effd3c0a8cc3718c999b7-588898cace7ed007c0a8b04a7a9843cf-8664b2fa77a7e089c0a8296870d1a409_fd40bdd0554bc033c0a8b09772af8fd3-56a2b546554aef3ec0a8b0976834b5b7-8664b2fa77a7e089c0a8296870d1a409/)

Request the handbook or manufacturer specification applicable to the offered vehicle, together with readable photographs of its identification and weight plates. Record the original sales market, model year, powertrain and fitted towing package. A brochure for a similarly named model in another country is a lead to investigate, not an automatic substitute.

GOV.UK says the towing limit is usually found in the handbook or specification sheet. Its car-towing guidance defines gross train weight as the fully loaded car and trailer together and advises against towing where the VIN plate does not show a train weight. Keep that advice within its UK context; elsewhere, obtain the relevant authority's answer rather than inventing an equivalent rating. [UK weight guidance](https://www.gov.uk/towing-with-car/weight-and-width-limits)

## Build a comparison that cannot hide a missing limit

Use separate rows for each constraint. A blank should remain a question for the manufacturer or suitably qualified destination specialist, not become an assumed allowance.

| Evidence requested | What it answers | Reason to pause acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Exact-vehicle manual and weight-plate photographs | Vehicle, combination and axle limits applicable to the offered unit | Seller supplies only a family-wide maximum |
| Braked and unbraked trailer limits, where specified | Which trailer configuration the rating covers | A braked rating is offered for an unbraked trailer |
| Permitted vertical coupling load | How much downward load the vehicle and hardware can accept | Only maximum trailer mass is documented |
| Towbar and coupling identification | Manufacturer, part number, rating and vehicle application | Label is missing or application cannot be matched |
| Installation record and vehicle-specific instructions | Whether the fitted components belong on this configuration | Unsupported fabrication or unexplained alterations |
| Loaded-vehicle and trailer measurements | Whether the actual planned combination fits its limits | Proposed load is based only on empty weights |
| Electrical and brake-function demonstration | Whether the installed system works with the intended trailer | A socket photograph is the only evidence |

Ford's US towing information distinguishes payload, vehicle weight, combination weight and axle ratings, and points buyers to VIN-based information and vehicle-specific guides. That is useful evidence of why several limits need checking, not permission to assign a US Ford rating to a different export vehicle. [Ford towing information](https://www.ford.com/towing/)

## Treat a retrofit as an installed system

Ask whether the towbar was factory fitted or added later. For a retrofit, request the supplier, part number, installation date, invoice and any associated wiring or control-module details. Have an appropriate installer explain how the instructions match the exact body, powertrain and production variant. The purpose is to make the claim testable before the vehicle leaves the seller.

Approval evidence also needs a location and an exception check. GOV.UK describes type-approved towbars as carrying an approval number and vehicle-applicability details. Its general approval requirement has an exception for cars first used before 1 August 1998. Do not turn that UK rule into a global condition, or discard the exception when discussing an older UK car. [UK towing-equipment guidance](https://www.gov.uk/towing-with-car/trailer-and-towing-equipment)

New South Wales guidance separately discusses suitable towbar and coupling capacities, lighting sockets and brake connections for trailers with power or electric brakes. Its marking guidance identifies the capacity, vehicle application or manufacturer's part number, and maker. These are useful items to photograph, but an overseas purchase still needs a destination-specific assessment. [NSW towing guidance](https://www.nsw.gov.au/driving-boating-and-transport/driving-nsw/roads-safety-and-rules/vehicle-safety-and-compliance/towing-a-caravan)

A connector adapter is not an acceptance test. Ask for a controlled demonstration with the intended trailer or an appropriate test setup, covering lighting and any required brake interface. Where the manufacturer specifies towing-related software, obtain evidence that it is installed and functioning. Volvo's cited XC90 instructions say a later towbar installation may require a system update; this is a model-specific example, not a claim that every vehicle needs coding.

## Compare the loaded combination, not two empty vehicles

Create the load plan before comparing ratings. Include the actual occupants, luggage, tools and accessories expected in the vehicle, and the trailer's intended cargo. Ask the inspector or weighing provider to identify what was loaded during each measurement. A weight ticket without that context cannot show whether the proposed working arrangement was tested.

The DVSA's safety guidance for England, Scotland and Wales distinguishes the car's own weight limit, its combined limit and both axle limits. It also calls for checks of the coupling, cable and lights. Use those distinct categories when discussing the measurements rather than accepting a single total as a complete answer. [DVSA trailer safety checks](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/tow-a-trailer-with-a-car-safety-checks)

Avoid creating a universal percentage for vertical coupling load from a generic online recommendation. Obtain the vehicle, towbar and trailer manufacturers' applicable instructions and ask a competent specialist to reconcile them. Likewise, do not assume that changing suspension parts or fitting a larger coupling increases the vehicle's certified limits.

## Make the seller's promise precise

For the purchase agreement, describe the actual configuration and evidence to be delivered. A useful commercial condition might identify the vehicle, the towbar part number, the intended trailer type, the relevant manufacturer documents and the acceptance demonstration. Have the parties agree who resolves missing information and whether acceptance remains open until it is supplied. This is a purchasing recommendation, not a substitute for legal advice on the contract.

There are three sensible outcomes. Accept the towing claim when the configuration and intended use are supported. Ask for clarification when a specific document or test is missing. Decline the claimed towing capability when the evidence contradicts it. A lower price does not repair an unresolved capacity or compatibility problem.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a fitted towbar prove that towing is permitted?

No. Establish the vehicle's applicable limits, the hardware's suitability and the destination's requirements separately. Physical fitment does not answer all three questions.

### Can a higher-rated towbar increase the car's towing limit?

Do not assume so. The vehicle remains subject to its own limits. Volvo's cited guidance explicitly distinguishes a towbar's rating from the car's permitted towing weight.

### Is a road test enough to accept the installation?

Not by itself. Request the relevant documents, inspection of the installation and a suitable electrical-function demonstration. A brief drive does not establish every weight limit or approval condition.

### Should a missing plate automatically cancel the purchase?

Pause the towing claim and seek authoritative clarification. The UK guidance gives specific advice about a missing train weight; other destinations need their own assessment. Do not replace missing evidence with a seller's estimate.
